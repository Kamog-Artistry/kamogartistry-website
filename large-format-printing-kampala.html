Materials & Finishing (Durability + Clean Mounting)

The quality of large format prints depends on the right material and proper finishing. Outdoor jobs need durability, while indoor jobs focus on clean appearance and smooth finishing.

Outdoor Durability

For outdoor banners and displays, we recommend options suited to sun/rain exposure and proper mounting.

Hemming & Eyelets

Proper hemming strengthens banner edges. Eyelets make mounting clean and reduce tearing.

Lamination (Where Needed)

Adds protection and improves longevity for some prints—especially high-contact or exposed surfaces.

Readability Setup

We advise on font sizing and spacing so your banner/poster is readable from the correct distance.
