To avoid delays, include these details

A procurement pack is easier to prepare when we know the exact purpose and format. A broad request like “send all company documents” can delay the response because some records are restricted.

Checklist or document list

Paste the list or attach the supplier registration checklist so we know what the pack should contain.

Purpose of the file

Tell us whether the pack is for supplier registration, tender submission, vendor onboarding, payment setup or internal review.

Submission format

Confirm whether the pack should be one PDF, separate files, signed forms, stamped copies or portal uploads.

How procurement packs are prepared

  1. We read the checklist first
    We confirm what your team actually needs instead of sending a generic bundle of files.
  2. We separate ordinary and restricted items
    Public profile items are treated differently from legal, finance and KYC records.
  3. We assemble the pack around your purpose
    The pack is prepared for the stated use: supplier registration, tender response, payment setup or portal onboarding.
  4. We share through the approved channel
    The file is sent through official email, supplier portal or verified procurement contact, depending on your instructions.

Procurement pack FAQs

When should we request a procurement pack?

Use this route when your checklist asks for several Kamog Artistry records together, such as a profile, registration details, tax or licence records, bank confirmation or KYC items.

What should we send with the request?

Send the checklist or portal instructions, the purpose of the request, supplier category, accepted file format, contact person and deadline.

Can the pack include restricted records?

Yes, where the checklist officially requires them and the requester is verified. Restricted legal, finance or KYC records are not shared as a default attachment.

What if we only need one document?

Use the Document Request route for one named record. A procurement pack is better when several records need to be assembled and submitted together.
