How tax and licence requests are handled

  1. We confirm the exact record
    State whether your team needs tax clearance, trading licence, TIN confirmation or both records.
  2. We check the purpose and deadline
    Tell us whether the record is for a tender, supplier file, finance setup, institutional review or portal upload.
  3. We verify the official channel
    Copies are shared through an official institutional email, supplier portal or approved procurement contact.
  4. We send only what was requested
    Tax and licence requests are kept focused so sensitive records are not overshared.

Tax clearance and trading licence FAQs

When should we use this page?

Use it when your checklist specifically asks for Kamog Artistry tax clearance, TIN confirmation or trading licence evidence.

What should we attach?

Attach the checklist line, tender instruction or portal requirement that mentions tax clearance or trading licence. This helps us send the correct record.

Can tax or licence records be uploaded to our portal?

Yes, where the portal instructions are clear and the request comes from an official institutional contact. Use the Vendor Onboarding Form for portal submissions.

Should bank or KYC records be requested here?

No. Use the Bank Details & Bank Reference route for finance records and the KYC route for director or due-diligence documents.
