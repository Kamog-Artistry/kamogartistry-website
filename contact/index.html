Details that help us respond faster

For print and branding jobs, include the item, size, quantity, material, finishing, artwork status, deadline and delivery point. For signage or installation, add the location, measurements and whether mounting or fabrication is required.

For proposals, RFQs and tenders, attach the brief, BOQ, TOR, drawings, artwork files or supplier checklist. For documents, mention the exact record, requester, purpose and deadline.

Pricing

Item, quantity, size, material, finishing, deadline, artwork status and delivery or installation location.

Procurement

Brief, RFQ, BOQ, TOR, drawings, evaluation format, submission deadline and official contact person.

Documents

Document name, purpose, requester organization, official email, portal link where applicable and deadline.

Contact FAQs

These answers help clients, procurement teams and project teams send requests with enough detail for a practical response.

Which contact page should I choose?

Use Request Quotation for pricing, Request Proposal for formal technical or financial responses, Tender / RFQ Upload for procurement files, Vendor Onboarding for supplier setup, Document Request for company records and Free 10-Minute Consult when you need guidance before submitting.

What should I include for a fast quotation?

Share the item, quantity, size, material, finishing, artwork status, deadline and delivery or installation location. For signage work, include site measurements or photos where possible.

Can I send artwork, RFQ or BOQ files?

Yes. Use the Tender / RFQ Upload page for RFQs, BOQs, TORs, drawings, artwork files and supplier checklists. For very large files, include a Drive or WeTransfer link.

Do you support procurement and institutional inquiries?

Yes. For supplier review or formal onboarding, use the Vendor Onboarding page together with our Corporate Profile, Capability Statement and Compliance pages.
