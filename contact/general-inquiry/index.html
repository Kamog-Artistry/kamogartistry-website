General Inquiry FAQs

When should I use General Inquiry?

Use it for open messages: service questions, follow-ups, partnership ideas, visit requests, portfolio questions, website feedback or anything not yet ready for quotation, proposal, RFQ upload, onboarding or document request.

Should I use this page for prices?

Only when the price request is still unclear. If you already know the item, size, quantity, material, finishing, deadline and location, use Request Quotation so pricing does not get delayed.

Can I attach screenshots or references?

Yes. Attach small screenshots, reference images, website-error screenshots, samples or previous conversation notes. For large artwork or tender folders, paste a Drive or WeTransfer link.

Can this page handle partnerships or referrals?

Yes. Use it for collaboration ideas, supplier introductions, media, case-study, backlink, portfolio or general business communication. For formal supplier setup, use Vendor Onboarding Form.
