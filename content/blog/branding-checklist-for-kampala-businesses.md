# Branding Checklist for Kampala Businesses

Starting or restructuring a business in Kampala?

Before hiring a designer or branding company, you need clarity.

This checklist helps you avoid expensive mistakes and ensures your brand is built professionally from day one.

## 1. Define Your Business Positioning

Before designing anything, answer:

- What problem do you solve?
- Who is your target audience?
- What makes you different?
- Are you premium, affordable, innovative, or traditional?

Without clarity, your branding will feel random.

If you need structured guidance, our [Brand Strategy Consulting Kampala](/services/branding-kampala/brand-strategy-consulting-kampala/) services help define positioning before design begins.

---

## 2. Logo Design (Not Just a Symbol)

Your logo must:

- Work in full color and black & white  
- Work on light and dark backgrounds  
- Be scalable (vector format)  
- Be supplied in AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, PNG formats  

Avoid:

- Low-resolution JPG logos  
- WhatsApp screenshots  
- Logos without source files  

For professional logo development, see our [Logo Design Kampala](/services/branding-kampala/logo-design-kampala/) service page.

---

## 3. Brand Identity System (Beyond the Logo)

Branding is not complete with just a logo.

You also need:

- Defined color codes (CMYK, RGB, HEX)  
- Font systems  
- Logo usage rules  
- Visual layout styles  
- Social media design consistency  

A proper identity system ensures your marketing looks consistent across signage, packaging, and print materials.

For structured identity development, explore our [Corporate Brand Identity Design Kampala](/services/branding-kampala/corporate-brand-identity-design-kampala/) services.

---

## 4. Brand Guidelines Document

Professional businesses require brand guidelines.

Your guide should include:

- Logo spacing rules  
- Minimum sizing  
- Approved color variations  
- Incorrect logo usage examples  
- Typography hierarchy  

Without guidelines, brand inconsistency quickly happens.

## 6. Print-Ready File Preparation

Many businesses make mistakes during printing.

Before sending files:

- Confirm correct size  
- Confirm 300 DPI  
- Ensure bleed is included  
- Export print-ready PDF  
- Confirm CMYK color mode
