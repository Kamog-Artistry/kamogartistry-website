## 6 Materials That Last in Uganda

Best long-term signage materials:
- ACP / Alucobond panels
- Acrylic with UV coating
- Cast vinyl (not cheap calendared vinyl)
- Laminated prints

Poor material choice leads to:
- Peeling
- Fading
- Warping
- Reprints
