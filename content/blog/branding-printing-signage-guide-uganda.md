## 1) Branding comes before printing

Printing only multiplies what already exists. If your branding is unclear, printing more will not fix it.

Before producing any materials, ensure you have:
- a clean logo (vector or high-resolution),
- consistent brand colors,
- selected fonts,
- a clear brand tone (formal, creative, bold, minimal).

**Common mistake:**  
Using different logo versions, colors, or fonts across flyers, banners, and business cards.

A consistent brand makes even simple printing look professional.

---

## 2) Printing quality: what actually matters

Clients often say *“I want quality”*, but quality is not just about ink.

True print quality includes:
- sharp text (no blur or pixelation),
- accurate colors,
- correct paper weight (GSM),
- neat trimming and finishing,
- durability for handling and storage.

### Paper selection (quick guide)
- **Letterheads / invoices:** 80–100gsm  
- **Flyers & brochures:** 130–170gsm  
- **Business cards:** 300–450gsm (laminated)  
- **Reports / profiles:** inner 100–130gsm, cover 250–300gsm  

Always ask your printer *what paper and finishing* will be used.

---

## 3) Signage & outdoor branding: material choice is critical

Uganda’s climate demands the right materials. Sun, dust, rain, and wind will quickly damage poor signage.

### Common signage options and when to use them
- **PVC banners:** cost-effective, short-to-medium term
- **ACM / Alucobond:** clean corporate signage, long-term
- **Acrylic signage:** premium indoor and outdoor branding
- **3D letters:** high visibility, professional storefronts
- **Vinyl graphics:** glass branding, vehicles, walls
- **One-way vision:** windows and offices

**Wrong material = wasted money.**

---

## 4) Visibility & placement (often overlooked)

A good design still fails if it cannot be read quickly.

Rule of thumb:
> A passer-by should understand your sign within **2–5 seconds**.

Checklist:
- large readable headline,
- strong contrast,
- short message,
- visible phone number or direction,
- correct mounting height.

Many signs fail because they try to say *everything* at once.

---

## 5) Timelines: realistic expectations

Production speed depends on:
- design readiness,
- approvals,
- material availability,
- installation requirements.

Typical timelines:
- Business cards & flyers: **1–3 days**
- Banners: **1–2 days**
- Shop signage: **3–10 days**
- 3D signage: **5–14 days**

Extremely fast promises often mean compromised quality.

---

## 6) Why cheap work becomes expensive

Low-cost work often leads to:
- fading colors,
- peeling vinyl,
- torn banners,
- reprints,
- replacements.

It’s cheaper long-term to:
✔ choose the correct material  
✔ ensure proper finishing  
✔ do it once, properly
