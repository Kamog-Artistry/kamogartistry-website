## Overview

Many printing problems don’t come from the printer — they come from the files.

Common complaints like:

- “my text is blurry”
- “colors changed”
- “the design looks stretched”
- “the edges were cut”

…often happen because the file was exported incorrectly.

This guide explains the best file formats and settings to get clean professional printing in Uganda.

## 1) Best File Format: PDF (Print Ready)

For most printing jobs, **PDF** is the best format because:

- it keeps text sharp,
- it keeps layout consistent,
- it preserves vector elements.

✅ Recommended export settings:
- PDF (High Quality Print)
- Embed fonts
- Include bleed if required
- Use CMYK color profile

## 2) AI / EPS (Best for Logos and Editable Design)

If you are sending a logo, always send:

- **AI**
- **EPS**
- **SVG**

These formats allow resizing without losing quality because they are vector-based.

Avoid sending logos as:
- WhatsApp screenshots
- Low-resolution JPG files
- Cropped PNGs

## 3) PNG / JPG (Only for Specific Uses)

PNG and JPG can work, but:

- they can become pixelated when enlarged,
- they don’t preserve vector sharpness,
- they may lose clarity during resizing.

Use PNG/JPG only when:

- the design is already high resolution (300 DPI),
- it’s for small prints,
- it’s primarily an image-based poster.

For important corporate materials like company profiles, brochures, and business cards, always use properly prepared print-ready PDFs via our [Printing Services Kampala](/services/printing-services-kampala/) hub.

## 4) Resolution: The 300 DPI Rule

For professional print quality, designs should be:

- **300 DPI** (especially posters, brochures, and product packaging)

Low resolution (72 DPI) may look fine on phone screens but prints blurry.

Before sending artwork for production, confirm resolution to avoid costly reprints.

## 5) Color Mode: CMYK vs RGB

Most screens display **RGB**.  
Most printing requires **CMYK**.

If you design in RGB:

- bright blues and greens may shift,
- vibrant tones may become dull in print.

Best practice:

- Design in CMYK from the start
- Or convert carefully before final export

## 6) Bleed and Safe Margins (To Avoid Cutting Problems)

Bleed = extra area around the design so trimming doesn’t cut important content.

Typical bleed:
- 3mm to 5mm (depends on job type)

Safe margin:
- Keep text and logos inside the safe area so they are not accidentally trimmed.

This is especially important for:
- Business cards
- Flyers
- Labels
- Packaging

## 7) Quick Checklist Before You Send Files to Print

Before sending your artwork:

✅ Confirm correct size  
✅ Confirm 300 DPI  
✅ Export as print-ready PDF  
✅ Ensure logos are vector (AI/EPS/SVG)  
✅ Include bleed if needed  
✅ Double-check spelling
