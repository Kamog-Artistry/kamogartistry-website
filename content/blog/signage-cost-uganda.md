## 1) What mainly affects signage cost?

Signage prices change based on:

- **Size** (bigger = more material)
- **Material type** (PVC, vinyl, acrylic, ACM, etc.)
- **Finishing** (frames, lamination, edges, LED lighting)
- **Installation** (height, wall type, distance, brackets)
- **Design readiness** (do you have artwork ready?)

---

## 2) Common signage types (and what they’re best for)

### A) PVC banners
Best for:
- promotions
- temporary signage
- events

Costs are mainly affected by:
- banner quality (thickness)
- finishing (eyelets vs framed)
- installation needs

---

### B) Vinyl (stickers, window branding, wall branding)
Best for:
- glass windows
- vehicles
- indoor branding

Costs are affected by:
- vinyl type (standard vs premium)
- lamination
- surface preparation
- complexity of installation

---

### C) ACM / Alucobond signage
Best for:
- corporate storefronts
- long-term outdoor signage

Costs depend on:
- board thickness
- printing method
- structure/frame requirements
- mounting method

---

### D) Acrylic signage
Best for:
- reception signage
- premium indoor/outdoor signage

Costs depend on:
- acrylic thickness
- cutting method
- standoffs/raised mounting
- print quality

---

### E) Lightbox signage
Best for:
- night visibility
- pharmacies, shops, brands with late hours

Costs depend on:
- size
- light system quality
- installation wiring and safety
- material durability

---

### F) 3D letters
Best for:
- premium storefronts
- professional offices

Costs depend on:
- letter size and thickness
- material (acrylic/PVC/metal)
- lighting (if any)
- installation complexity

---

## 3) Budget tips (how to save without losing quality)

To save money smartly:
- use the right material for the job (don’t overpay)
- reduce unnecessary size
- simplify design text
- choose durable finishing once instead of repeating prints

“Cheap” work often becomes expensive due to replacements.
