How Our Order Tracking Works

We update your order as it moves through design, proofing, production, finishing, pickup or delivery.

1) Order Received

Your request is recorded with a unique order reference number after details such as item, quantity, artwork and delivery needs are confirmed.

2) Production Updates

Your status may show artwork/design stage, client approval, pre-press checks, production, finishing and quality control depending on the job.

3) Pickup or Delivery

Once ready, your order may show ready for pickup, pickup scheduled, out for delivery, delivered or completed with relevant notes.

Privacy note: for safety, this page only displays basic order tracking details. Full payment records, private phone details and internal production notes are not displayed publicly.
