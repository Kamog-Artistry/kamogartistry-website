Rain Challenge During Vehicle Branding

This short clip shows a real field moment from the All Parents Primary School Mubende branding job, when rain interrupted the evening installation work.

Request Vehicle Branding Quote View Full Case Study

About this field challenge

Vehicle branding needs clean, dry conditions for proper application. During this project, rain interrupted the installation and the team had to pause the work.

What the video shows

The clip captures the practical side of on-site branding: weather can affect timing, surface preparation and installation flow, especially when work continues into evening hours.

How the project continued

The installation was handled carefully and completed properly after the interruption, keeping the final school van branding clean and presentable.
