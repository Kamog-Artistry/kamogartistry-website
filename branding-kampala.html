Our Branding Process (Clear & Reliable)

1) Brief & Brand Direction

You share what you do, target audience, competitors, and what you want your brand to feel like (premium, affordable, corporate, youthful, etc.).

2) Concepts & Refinement

We develop brand concepts, refine based on feedback, and ensure the identity works in real applications like print and signage.

3) Deliverables & Brand Kit

You receive final files (print + web formats) and a brand kit with guidelines, colors and usage rules for consistent branding.

4) Implementation (Optional)

We implement across printing, signage and branded items—so your brand becomes visible immediately across Kampala.

What You Receive (Deliverables)

Deliverables depend on your package, but typically include:

  • Logo files: SVG/PDF (print), PNG (transparent), JPG (web), and variants (color/mono).
  • Brand colors: CMYK/RGB/HEX values for printing and digital consistency.
  • Typography guidance: recommended fonts and usage rules.
  • Stationery designs: business card, letterhead, envelope (optional).
  • Social templates: optional branded templates for campaigns.
  • Print-ready setup: files prepared correctly for printing/signage production in Uganda.

Pricing Factors

Cost depends on: brand scope (logo vs full identity), number of applications, revision rounds, and urgency. For corporate packages, document count and profile length also affect pricing.

Timeline Factors

Timeline depends on feedback speed and scope. We confirm delivery schedule after reviewing your brief and required outputs.
