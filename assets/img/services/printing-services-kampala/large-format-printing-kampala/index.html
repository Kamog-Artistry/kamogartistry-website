Our Large Format Printing Process

To avoid waste and reprints, we follow a proof-first workflow: confirm specs, correct the artwork, approve a proof, then produce and finish for installation.

1) Specs & Use-Case

You share size, quantity, placement (indoor/outdoor), deadline, and where it will be installed. We advise material + finishing.

2) Artwork Check

We check resolution, bleed, scaling, and color. If the file is weak, we fix setup to prevent pixelation and cropping.

3) Proof & Approval

You approve the final proof (layout, spelling, sizing notes). This step is what protects you from expensive mistakes.

4) Print, Finish & Delivery

We print, finish (hemming/eyelets/lamination where required) and prepare it for clean mounting or transport.

Technical Specs We Print For

Large format quality is decided before the printer starts: correct resolution, correct scale, correct bleed, and the right finishing for installation. If you’re not sure, send what you have—we’ll guide the right setup.

Resolution & Scale

We advise the right resolution for the viewing distance and ensure artwork is scaled correctly (so text stays crisp and images don’t pixelate).

Bleed & Safe Margins

We add/confirm bleed for trims and keep key text/logos within safe zones—especially for posters, frames and mounted graphics.

Materials & Finishing

Indoor vs outdoor material choices matter. We recommend finishing like hemming, eyelets, lamination or mounting based on use.

Color Consistency

We aim for consistent outputs across reprints by keeping your files clean, aligned and properly exported for large format production.

Common Large Format Mistakes We Prevent

Most printing disappointments come from file setup and finishing choices—not the printer. These are the common issues we fix before production so you don’t waste money.

Pixelation

Low-res images stretched to banner size, resulting in blurry faces, logos and text.

Wrong Size

Artwork not scaled correctly (e.g., roll-up content cropped or backdrop logos too small).

No Bleed

Posters trimmed without bleed, causing white edges or cutting into text.

Weak Finishing

Outdoor banners without proper hemming/eyelets tear quickly in wind and handling.

Large Format Printing Kampala — FAQs

What do you need to quote large format printing accurately?

Size (W×H), quantity, usage (indoor/outdoor), finishing (eyelets/hemming/lamination/mounting), deadline, and delivery location. If you have artwork, share it—if not, we can design or fix setup.

Can you print urgent banners and event backdrops?

Yes, depending on size, quantity and workload. The fastest jobs are those with clear specs and quick proof approval. For full event support, use Event Branding.

My artwork is low quality—can you still print?

We’ll first check the file. If it’s too low-res for the size, we’ll recommend redesign, re-setting, or using suitable alternatives to avoid blurry output.

Do you help with installation or mounting?

Yes—finishing and preparation depend on where it will be installed. For signage builds and fabrication, see Signage Fabrication.

Which is better: roll-ups or banners?

Roll-ups are ideal for indoor portable marketing and repeated setups. Banners are better for outdoor visibility and larger coverage. Tell us the use-case and we’ll advise the best option.

Do you deliver around Kampala and nearby areas?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and nearby areas. Share your location and deadline for delivery planning.

Can you handle design and printing together?

Yes. If you need artwork created or corrected for large format specs, we can handle it end-to-end. See Graphic Design Kampala.
