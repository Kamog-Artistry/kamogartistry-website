FAQ (Branding, Printing & Signage in Kampala)

Quick answers to common questions clients ask before requesting a quote.

Do you offer branding, printing and signage as one package in Kampala?

Yes. We deliver end-to-end work—from design and proofing to print production, signage fabrication and installation (when required).

How long does printing take in Kampala?

Most standard printing takes 1–3 days depending on quantity and finishing. We confirm timelines after file checks and approval.

What do you need to quote signage?

Share your location, preferred size, deadline, and a photo/video of the installation area. Include reference designs/brand colors if available.

Do you design files for clients who don’t have artwork?

Yes. We design from scratch or improve what you have, then prepare print-ready files (correct size, bleed, resolution) to avoid reprints.

Which areas do you serve besides Kampala?

We primarily serve Kampala and Wakiso, and also take projects in Mukono and across Uganda depending on scope and installation needs.
