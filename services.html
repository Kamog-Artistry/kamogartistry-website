How We Work

We keep production practical: confirm the details, guide the material choice, share a proof, then produce or install after approval.

1. Confirm the job

We check the item type, size, quantity, deadline, artwork status and delivery or installation location.

2. Guide the material

We advise paper, PVC, vinyl, acrylic, reflective film or finishing options before production starts.

3. Share proof

We confirm layout, spellings, sizes, bleed, safe margins and logo placement before printing or fabrication.

4. Produce and deliver

We execute the approved work, package it well and plan delivery or installation where required.

FAQ

Quick answers before starting a branding, printing, signage or production job with Kamog Artistry (U) Ltd.

Do you offer branding, printing and signage as one package in Kampala?

Yes. We can handle design, proofing, print production, signage fabrication and installation where required. Start with Branding Kampala if the job needs one consistent brand direction.

How long does printing take in Kampala?

Most standard print jobs take 1–3 working days after artwork approval, depending on quantity and finishing. Check Printing Services Kampala for common print routes.

What do you need to quote signage or installation?

Send the size, location, wall or mounting surface, preferred material, deadline and a photo of the site. For signage routes, start at Signage & Branding Kampala.

Do you handle repeat corporate stationery orders?

Yes. We produce letterheads, envelopes, stamps, invoice books, receipt books, folders and repeat office print items. Visit Corporate Stationery.

Can you help if I do not have print-ready artwork?

Yes. We can design, clean up, resize or prepare artwork with bleed, safe margins and a production proof before printing.

Can you advise the right material before production?

Yes. Use our Materials & Finishing Options page or request material guidance before quotation.
