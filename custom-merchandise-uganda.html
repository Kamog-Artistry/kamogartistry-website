Our Process (Brief → Proof → Produce → Pack → Deliver)

We keep bulk and tender orders predictable: clarify specs, approve proofs, then produce and pack consistently.

1) Confirm Items + Quantities

Share your list, quantities, sizes (for apparel), deadline and delivery location.

2) Recommend Branding Method

We recommend screen print, embroidery, DTF or sublimation depending on item type and finish.

3) Layouts + Proofs

We confirm placement (front/back/sleeve), sizes, colour approach and finishing expectations.

4) Production + QC

We check consistency and alignment across bulk quantities—this is where quality is felt.

5) Packing + Labelling

Neat packing for distribution; we can bundle by department/size where needed.

6) Delivery

Delivery in Kampala and nearby districts (Wakiso/Mukono) depending on schedule; nationwide by scope.

What to Send for a Fast Quote (Corporate & Tender Orders)

To quote accurately and reduce delays, send:

  • Items list: which merchandise you want (e.g., t-shirts + caps + umbrellas + notebooks)
  • Quantities: per item and per variation
  • Branding method: screen print / embroidery / DTF / sublimation (or ask us to recommend)
  • Branding placement: chest / sleeve / back / single side / two sides
  • Colours: logo colours + number of print colours (if known)
  • Apparel sizes: S/M/L/XL/XXL breakdown (for uniforms, overalls, t-shirts)
  • Packaging level: simple pack vs premium pack (gift sets + bags + inserts)
  • Deadline: event date or submission date
  • Delivery location: Kampala / Wakiso / Mukono / other
  • Budget range: optional but helps us propose realistic options

Custom Merchandise Uganda FAQs

Which method is best: screen print, embroidery, DTF or sublimation?

It depends on the item and finish required. Embroidery is premium and durable for uniforms/caps/overalls. Screen printing is strong for bulk tees. DTF handles detailed multi-color transfers. Sublimation suits compatible items for clean full-color results.

Do you handle bulk corporate orders and tender supply?

Yes. We support procurement teams with clear specs, proof approvals and consistent production across bulk quantities.

Can you supply complete conference packs?

Yes—common combinations include notebooks + pens + packaging bags + mugs, packed for distribution.

How do you quote uniforms and overalls?

We confirm garment type, size breakdown, branding placement, and method (often embroidery) then quote with options.

Can you include packaging and printed inserts?

Yes. We can support packaging bags and printed inserts/tags/cards to improve presentation.

How fast can you deliver?

Turnaround depends on items, quantities, branding method and approval speed. After confirming details, we provide a reliable timeline.
