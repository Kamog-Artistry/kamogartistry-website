Brand Guidelines That Keep Your Business Consistent

Brand guidelines are the “rulebook” that makes your brand repeatable. They explain exactly how to use your logo, what colors to use (and how), what fonts to use, and how layouts should look—so different people can create materials without breaking your brand.

Guidelines are especially important if you use multiple suppliers in Kampala (printers, signage fabricators, uniform suppliers, digital marketers). What You Receive (Practical Brand Manual Deliverables)

Your guidelines are designed to be usable—not “theory.” We include real examples, correct codes, and clear do’s and don’ts that teams and vendors can follow quickly.

Logo Usage Rules

Clear guidance so your logo is never stretched, misplaced or cluttered.

  • Primary + secondary logo versions
  • Clear space and minimum size
  • Correct placement on backgrounds
  • Incorrect usage examples (do not do)

Color System (Vendor-Ready Codes)

Exact codes for print and digital to reduce color drift across vendors.

  • CMYK for printing
  • RGB/HEX for screens
  • Primary, secondary and neutral palette
  • Usage guidance (what goes where)

Typography + Layout Standards

Fonts and hierarchy rules that keep proposals, profiles and marketing clean and corporate.

  • Headline + body font pairing
  • Sizes, spacing and hierarchy rules
  • Document styling direction
  • Optional template guidance

Our Brand Guidelines Process

We keep the process simple: understand how you use the brand, define the rules, show real examples, then deliver a clean manual your team can follow. This reduces repeated design costs and prevents brand drift.

1) Audit & Use-Cases

We review what you have (logo files, colors, documents, social posts) and how you use the brand: documents, signage, uniforms, packaging and digital.

2) Define the Standards

We set logo rules, color codes, typography and layout rules that will remain consistent across suppliers and platforms.

3) Build Real Examples

We show correct usage examples (and incorrect ones) so teams understand instantly—especially helpful for vendors.

4) Delivery + Rollout Support

You receive a well-structured guidelines document plus the correct asset package. Optional rollout support helps you implement across print and signage.

Common Brand Consistency Mistakes We Fix in Kampala

Most brand damage happens during execution. These are the common issues we see when there’s no clear guidelines manual.

Stretched Logos

Vendors resize incorrectly, distort proportions, and the logo loses professionalism.

Wrong Colors

Different printers guess shades, causing inconsistent prints, signage and uniforms.

Random Fonts

Teams use different fonts in documents and posts, making the brand look inconsistent.

No Layout Rules

Every brochure or profile looks like a new brand because spacing and hierarchy are not defined.

Brand Guidelines Design Kampala — FAQs

What are brand guidelines?

Brand guidelines are a manual that defines how your brand should look: logo usage, colors, fonts, layouts, and real examples. They help staff, designers and vendors keep everything consistent over time.

Do I need guidelines if I already have a logo?

Yes—especially if different people handle printing, signage or social media. Guidelines stop stretching, wrong colors and random fonts. If the logo itself needs improvement, see Logo Design.

What is included in a brand guidelines document?

Typically: logo rules (clear space/min size), color codes (CMYK/RGB/HEX), typography, layout guidance, examples, and do’s/don’ts. For a full identity system, see Corporate Brand Identity.

How much do brand guidelines cost in Kampala?

Cost depends on brand complexity, number of pages, examples, templates included, and rollout use-cases like signage, uniforms or packaging. See Branding Pricing for common scopes.

Can guidelines include templates for proposals and company documents?

Yes. We can include document styling guidance and template direction so profiles, proposals and letters stay consistent. For implementation support, see Brand Rollout Management.

Will printers and signage vendors in Kampala understand the manual?

Yes—because we design the manual with practical vendor use in mind (clear codes, simple rules, and real examples). For production services, see Printing and Signage.

Can you update or modernize existing guidelines?

Yes. We can refine your rules, fix gaps, and modernize layouts and examples—especially useful after a refresh. If you’re changing the whole identity direction, see Rebranding Services.

How do we start a brand guidelines project?

Share your logo files (or what you have), how you use the brand, and which vendors you work with. Start via Contact or WhatsApp for a quick review.
