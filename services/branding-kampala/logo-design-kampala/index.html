Our Logo Design Process

We keep the process clear and efficient: direction first, then design, then refinement, then a clean final delivery package. This reduces confusion and makes approvals faster.

1) Brief & Direction

We align on what you do, target audience, competitors, and where the logo must work (signage, packaging, uniforms, etc.).

2) Concepts

We develop clean directions with strong legibility and brand meaning—built for real use, not just “pretty visuals.”

3) Refinement

We refine the chosen direction: proportions, spacing, typography, balance, and small-size readability.

4) Final Files & Handover

You receive organised production-ready exports, plus usage notes so printers and vendors don’t distort your logo.

Common Logo Mistakes We Fix in Kampala

Many logos fail not because the idea is bad, but because they are not prepared for production. Here are the most common issues we see—and avoid.

Pixelation

Using a screenshot/low-res image that becomes blurry in print and signage.

Wrong Files

Only PNG/JPG provided—vendors need vector files (AI/EPS/PDF) for clean output.

Too Detailed

Tiny details disappear on stamps, labels, embroidery, and social icons.

No System

A logo without colors/typography rules leads to inconsistent branding over time.

Logo Design Kampala — FAQs

How much does logo design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on scope (simple logo vs multiple variations), number of concepts, revision cycles, urgency, and production use-cases like signage, packaging and uniforms. For common scopes, see Branding Pricing.

What files will I receive for my logo?

You receive vector files (AI/EPS/PDF) for professional printing and signage, plus web formats (PNG/JPG) and optional SVG for digital UI usage. This prevents pixelation and vendor confusion.

Do you design logos for startups and small businesses?

Yes. For launch-ready scopes, check Startup Branding Packages. For growing businesses, see SME Brand Development.

How long does a professional logo take?

Timeline depends on brief clarity and feedback speed. A standard project is faster when direction is clear. If you need help defining direction, begin with Brand Strategy Consulting.

Can you redesign or vectorize an existing logo?

Yes. We can refine, redraw and prepare it as a clean vector with proper variations and export formats—useful when the current logo is blurry or inconsistent in print. If the business is changing direction, see Rebranding Services.

Do you provide brand guidelines after logo design?

Yes. Guidelines help your team and vendors maintain consistency. Explore Brand Guidelines Design or a full Corporate Identity System.

Can you handle printing and brand rollout too?

Yes. We can implement your logo across print and signage to keep everything consistent. See Brand Rollout Management.
