Our Packaging Design Process

We start with the dieline and product info, then design for shelf clarity, then refine for print and finishing—so you avoid expensive reprints.

1) Brief + Content

We collect product details, variants, claims, ingredients/instructions, contacts and any required blocks.

2) Dieline & Constraints

You provide a dieline/template from the printer. We confirm folds, glue areas, panel order and safe zones.

3) Design + Refinement

We design the full pack layout, then refine typography, spacing and readability to avoid clutter and printing issues.

4) Final Exports

We deliver press-ready files and approval previews organized for your vendor to print immediately.

Common Packaging Mistakes We Fix in Kampala

Packaging can be expensive to reprint. Here are the common mistakes we avoid so your first production run comes out right.

Wrong Panel Order

Design placed on the wrong dieline panels—front/back end up swapped after folding.

No Safe Margins

Text and logos too close to trim/folds—gets cut or distorted in production.

Cluttered Front

Too many messages compete; product name and key benefit aren’t obvious.

Inconsistent Variants

Each SKU looks like a different brand—reduces trust and shelf impact.

Packaging Design Kampala — FAQs

Do you design packaging for boxes, pouches and cartons in Kampala?

Yes. We design packaging for boxes, pouches, sleeves and cartons, aligned to real dielines and prepared for printing and finishing.

Do I need a dieline before you start?

Ideally, yes. A dieline from your printer ensures panel sizes, folds and glue areas are correct. If you don’t have one yet, share your pack dimensions and we’ll advise the next step.

Can you design packaging that matches my brand identity?

Yes. We align packaging to your logo, colors and typography. If you need consistency rules, add Brand Guidelines.

Do you also design labels for bottles and jars?

Yes. For matching labels, see Product Label Design.

Will you include bleed and trimming safety on packaging files?

Yes. We include bleed and safe margins to avoid trimming errors and keep important content away from folds.

Can you help with printing and finishing recommendations?

Yes. We can advise on finishes like matte/gloss lamination, spot UV and foil, and help coordinate production-ready delivery via Printing Services.

How much does packaging design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on pack complexity, number of variants, and finishing/print requirements. For guidance, see Branding Pricing.

How do we start a packaging design project?
