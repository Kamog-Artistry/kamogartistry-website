We keep startup branding efficient: clarity first, then design, then refinement, then a clean delivery package that vendors can use immediately.

Common Startup Branding Mistakes in Kampala

Many startups lose trust because the brand feels inconsistent. Here are common problems we help you avoid from the start.

Random Visuals

Different fonts, colors and styles across posts and documents—no system.

Low-Res Files

Only screenshots/PNGs provided, causing blurry printing and signage output.

Over-Designed Logos

Too many details that disappear on stamps, labels and small icons.

No Rollout Plan

Brand looks good once, but no guidance exists for consistency as the team grows.

Startup Branding Packages Kampala — FAQs

What’s included in a startup branding package?

Typically: logo + essential variations, a mini identity system (colors and typography), and starter templates. For broader systems, upgrade to Corporate Brand Identity.

How much do startup branding packages cost in Kampala?

Cost depends on scope, number of deliverables, revision rounds, urgency and where the brand must work (signage, packaging, uniforms). See Branding Pricing for guidance.

Do you provide the correct files for printing and signage?

Yes. We provide vector files (AI/EPS/PDF) plus web formats (PNG/JPG) to prevent pixelation and vendor issues. This matters for print, signage and embroidery in Kampala.

Can we start small and upgrade later?
