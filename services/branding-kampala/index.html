Our Branding Process

1) Brand Discovery

We clarify your audience, offers, competitors, location, use-cases and what the brand must achieve.

2) Strategy & Direction

We define positioning, visual tone, message priorities and the identity route before designing.

3) Identity System

Logo, colours, typography, layout rules and visual elements are built for print and digital use.

4) Applications

We apply the system to selected items such as stationery, packaging, profiles, signage or templates.

5) Review & Refinement

Feedback is consolidated, refined and checked against business goals and production needs.

6) Export & Rollout

You receive correct final files, usage guidance and optional rollout support for suppliers.

Branding Kampala — FAQs

What is the best branding company in Kampala for SMEs?

The best fit is a branding company that can handle more than a logo: strategy, identity design, guidelines, print-ready files and rollout. Kamog Artistry focuses on practical brand systems for SMEs, NGOs, institutions and companies in Kampala.

What is the difference between logo design and full branding?

A logo is one mark. Full branding includes the full system around it: colours, typography, layouts, messaging direction, stationery, packaging, signage and digital templates. See Corporate Brand Identity and Brand Guidelines.

How much does branding cost in Kampala?

Branding cost depends on whether you need a logo only, a full identity, packaging, labels, social templates, brand guidelines or rollout support. Visit Branding Pricing or request a tailored quote.

How long does branding take?

A logo-only project is usually faster than a complete corporate identity. Full systems take longer because they include applications, guidelines and export checks. Strategy-led projects can start with Brand Strategy Consulting.

What files do we receive after branding?

Depending on scope, you can receive print-ready PDFs, web-ready images, vector logo files, colour values, font guidance, templates and brand guideline documents.

Do you design brand guidelines?

Yes. Brand guidelines cover logo usage, spacing, colours, typography, incorrect usage, layout examples and practical application rules. Explore Brand Guidelines Design Kampala.

Can you rebrand an existing business?

Yes. We can refresh or fully reposition an existing brand while protecting recognition where needed. See Rebranding Services Kampala.

Do you provide packaging and product label design?

Yes. We design packaging and labels for product visibility, readable information hierarchy and print production. See Packaging Design and Product Label Design.

Can you implement the brand after design?

Yes. We can support rollout across printing, signage, packaging, vehicle branding and digital templates through Brand Rollout Management.

Do you work with NGOs and corporate procurement teams?

Yes. We support SMEs, NGOs, institutions and corporates. Procurement teams can review the Corporate Profile and Compliance pages.

Do you serve businesses outside Kampala?

Yes. We serve Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and wider Uganda depending on project type and delivery needs.
