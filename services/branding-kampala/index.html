Branding Kampala — FAQs

What’s the difference between a logo and full branding?

A logo is a single mark. Branding is the full system around it—colors, typography, messaging, layout rules and how everything looks across documents, signage, packaging and digital. If you need a complete system, see Corporate Brand Identity and Brand Guidelines.

How much does branding cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on scope (logo only vs full identity), number of deliverables, revision rounds, urgency, and where the brand will be used (stationery, documents, packaging, signage). For packaging-heavy projects, see Packaging Design and Label Design. You can also request a quote on the Contact page.

How long does a logo or full corporate identity take?

Timelines depend on scope and feedback speed. A logo can be delivered faster; a full identity takes longer because it includes guidelines and multiple applications. If you’re starting from scratch, begin with Brand Strategy Consulting.

What deliverables do we receive after branding is complete?

You receive print-ready and web-ready logo files, identity specs (colors, fonts), and—where included—templates and a practical guide so your team stays consistent. See our Portfolio for finished examples and your vendor-ready output.

Do you provide brand guidelines?

Yes. We produce practical guidelines covering logo usage, spacing, color codes, typography, do’s and don’ts, and examples for common applications. Explore Brand Guidelines Design Kampala.

Can you handle printing, signage or vehicle branding as part of implementation?

Yes. We can implement the identity across print and signage so everything remains consistent—from business cards and brochures to storefront signage, banners and vehicle branding. See Printing, Signage, and Vehicle Branding.

Do you work with startups, SMEs and NGOs?

Yes. We support startups, SMEs, NGOs and corporates. For early-stage launch kits, see Startup Branding Packages. For growing businesses, see SME Brand Development.

Share your business name, what you do, target audience, and what you need (logo, identity, stationery, profile, packaging, signage). Start here: Contact. Corporate procurement teams can review Corporate Profile and Compliance.
