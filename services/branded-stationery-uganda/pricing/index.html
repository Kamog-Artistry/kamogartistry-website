What Affects Stationery Cost in Kampala

1) Paper & Material

80gsm vs 100gsm/120gsm bond paper, thick card stock for folders, and specialty textures affect price.

2) Colour & Coverage

Single-colour is cheaper; full colour or heavy ink coverage costs more. Double-sided printing increases cost.

3) Books & Sets

Duplicate/triplicate sets (carbonless) and number of sets per book influence material and finishing time.

4) Numbering & Control

Sequential numbering, controlled ranges, and extra QA steps increase setup and production time.

5) Finishing

Perforation, padding, stitching, lamination, spot UV, emboss and die-cuts affect production steps.

6) Turnaround & Delivery

Rush deadlines may require priority scheduling. Delivery cost depends on location and volume.

Branded Stationery Uganda Pricing — FAQs

What do you need to quote invoice/receipt books accurately?

Size (A4/A5/A6), number of sets (2-part/3-part), sets per book, numbering requirement, and whether you have a sample. Use the Fast Quote Checklist.

Can you match an existing layout exactly?

Yes. Share a photo or scan of your current book (cover and inner page). We can match the format and improve clarity where needed.

Do prices include design?

If you have an existing layout, minor adjustments are usually straightforward. Full redesign is quoted based on complexity.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and nationwide delivery options. Share delivery location and deadline for planning.

How fast can stationery be produced?

Simple items like business cards and letterheads are faster. Books and folders take longer due to numbering and finishing. If urgent, WhatsApp your quantities and deadline.

Can you standardize documents across departments?

Yes. We can standardize layouts, numbering ranges, and department identifiers for controlled use—ideal for institutions.

Do you provide VAT invoices and procurement documentation?

Yes. For tender support, refer to Corporate Profile and Compliance.

How do we start an order?

Start on Contact or WhatsApp. Upload your logo/sample and share your quantities.
