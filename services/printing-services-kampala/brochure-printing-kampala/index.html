Where brochures work best

Service brochures for clinics, schools, NGOs, hotels and tours; product brochures for distributors and retail brands; and corporate brochures for boardroom meetings, tenders and stakeholder briefings—where print quality directly affects credibility.

Brochure Formats We Print

We help you choose a format based on content length, distribution (handouts vs meetings), and how you want the reader to flow through your message.

Bi-Fold Brochures (Half-Fold)

Clean and professional. Great when you need larger images, less clutter, and a strong “presentation” feel.

  • Best for: corporate introductions, product highlights
  • Stronger readability (more space per panel)
  • Works well with premium paper + matte finishing

Tri-Fold & Z-Fold Brochures

Ideal for structured messaging: services, steps, pricing ranges, or multi-section offers.

  • Best for: service menus, tourism, clinics, campaigns
  • 6 panels for clear segmentation
  • Common for handouts and events

Booklet-Style Brochures (Multi-Page)

When content is bigger than a fold. Great for proposals, programs, training guides, and product catalogs.

  • Best for: catalogs, programs, proposals, training packs
  • Neat binding options (e.g., saddle stitch)
  • Premium “document” feel for boardrooms

Finishing Options (Most Requested)

Matte or gloss lamination for durability, optional spot UV highlights for premium impact (best used selectively on logos or headings), and clean scoring for folds so papers don’t crack on the fold line. For long-lasting distribution, lamination is often recommended. For advanced finishes and binding support, see Print Finishing Services.

Our Brochure Printing Process (Proof-First)

Brochures fail when artwork is rushed. We run a proof-first workflow to protect your budget and reputation.

1) Confirm Format & Quantity

We confirm brochure type (bi-fold, tri-fold, z-fold, booklet), quantity, size, and deadline—then recommend paper and finishing options.

2) Artwork Check

We check margins, panel order, folds, image quality, and print settings (CMYK export, correct bleed and safe zones).

3) Proof Approval

You approve the proof before printing. This prevents wrong contacts, wrong panel flow, and spelling mistakes.

4) Print, Finish & Deliver

We print, score/fold accurately, finish as selected, and package for distribution. Delivery can be arranged across Kampala, Wakiso and Mukono.

Print Specs That Keep Brochures Looking Premium

Professional brochures depend on correct setup: paper weight, bleed/margins, fold scoring, and readable hierarchy.

Paper Weight Selection

Folded brochures often need stronger stock than flyers so they don’t feel flimsy. We recommend paper based on distribution method and durability needs.

Bleed + Safe Areas

We set up bleed and safe margins so backgrounds reach the edge and important text stays away from trimming and fold lines.

Correct Panel Flow

Tri-folds and z-folds have a specific panel order. We verify the reader flow so sections don’t land “in the wrong place.”

Booklet Page Rules

For saddle-stitched booklet-style brochures, page counts must align with folding sets (practical rule: multiples of 4) for clean binding.

Paper & finishing recommendations (by use-case)

If you’re unsure what “looks premium” for your audience, we guide you based on how the brochure will be used and how long it should last.

  • High-volume handouts: practical paper + clean fold scoring; focus on clear headings and scan-friendly layout.
  • Boardroom / procurement: stronger stock + matte lamination for a corporate feel; consistent typography and white space.
  • Tourism / hospitality: image-led layout + gloss/matte protection for repeated handling.
  • Product brochures: structured sections and pricing tables; consider booklet-style if SKUs are many.

Artwork Setup for Brochure Printing (Avoid Costly Reprints)

If you already have artwork, send a print-ready PDF (preferred). If you are designing in Canva/Corel/Illustrator/InDesign, we’ll guide export settings so colours and trims behave correctly.

Preferred File Type

PDF (High Quality Print). If you have open files, include fonts or convert text to outlines where appropriate.

Colour Mode

Export in CMYK (not RGB) to reduce colour shifts. We can also advise when brand colours need closer matching.

Bleed & Safe Zones

Include bleed on backgrounds and keep key text/logos away from edges and fold lines to avoid trimming issues.

Images & Resolution

Use sharp images; low-resolution photos will print soft and reduce trust. We can help replace or enhance images where needed.

Common Brochure Mistakes We Prevent

Most “bad brochures” come from rushed design decisions. We fix the issues that make brochures look cheap or confusing.

Wrong Panel Order

Tri-fold content placed in the wrong panels causes an awkward reading sequence.

Crowded Layout

Too much text with weak spacing makes brochures hard to scan and reduces conversions.

Low-Res Images

Pixelated photos destroy trust. We advise image quality and print-ready exports.

Weak Paper Choice

Flimsy paper lowers perceived value. We match paper weight to purpose and distribution.

Brochure Printing Kampala — FAQs

What do you need to quote brochure printing?

Brochure format (bi-fold/tri-fold/z-fold/booklet), size, quantity, finish (matte/gloss/lamination), deadline, and delivery location. If artwork is ready, attach a print-ready PDF for faster confirmation.

What’s the difference between a brochure and a booklet?

A brochure is usually a single sheet folded (bi-fold, tri-fold, etc.). A booklet is multiple pages bound together and suits longer content. For booklet-style brochures, see Book Printing.

Which brochure format is best for services?

Tri-fold and z-fold are great for structured messaging (steps, packages, service categories). Bi-fold suits more presentation-style content. If you have many services or SKUs, consider Catalog Printing.

Can you help with brochure design before printing?

Yes. We can create or refine your brochure layout for clarity and professional hierarchy. See Graphic Design Kampala.

Do you print brochures for events and NGOs?

Yes—events, campaigns, programs, product guides and corporate handouts. If you also need event branding items, see Event Branding Kampala.

Do you deliver around Kampala?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and nearby areas. Share your location and deadline for planning.
