Paper Options for Brochures (What Most Clients Choose)

Paper selection affects price, feel, and how your brochure performs in real use (handling, folding, durability). If you are unsure, we recommend paper based on your purpose: mass promotion vs corporate presentation vs premium brand brochure.

Art Paper (Gloss / Matte)

Common for full-color brochures. Produces strong image quality and clean colors. Often used for bi-fold and tri-fold brochures. Typical choices: 130–170 GSM (promo) or 200–250 GSM (premium feel).

Bond Paper (Office / Informational)

Suitable for informational brochures with less photography. Often used when clients want a writable surface (notes, forms). Typical choices: 80–120 GSM.

Higher GSM / Premium Feel

Thicker paper feels more corporate and improves durability. Recommended when brochures represent premium services or procurement presentations. Most premium brochure builds are 200 GSM+ with optional matte lamination.

Fold Safety

Thin paper can crack less but feels “light”; thicker paper benefits from correct folding technique and optional creasing (scoring) to avoid fold cracks.

Finishing Add-ons That Affect Brochure Pricing

Most brochure quotes differ based on finishing. For corporate brochures, finishing improves durability and presentation. For mass promotions, finishing is optional depending on budget.

Lamination (Gloss / Matte)

Protects against moisture and handling. Matte is common for corporate materials; gloss adds pop for bright promotions.

Folding Accuracy

Tri-fold and gate-fold brochures require correct panel widths and safe margins. Poor folding ruins alignment and readability.

Creasing / Scoring

Reduces cracking on folds (especially on thicker paper and heavy ink coverage). Recommended for premium brochures and dark-solid designs.

Special Trimming

Rounded corners or custom shapes increase finishing steps. Best for premium brand brochures and special campaigns.

Design / Artwork Preparation

If the artwork is not print-ready, design support may be required. We correct margins, bleed, and fold-safe panel setup before printing. See Graphic Design.

Delivery Planning

Bulk quantities may require packaging and delivery coordination (Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono). Share location early for accurate logistics.

What Determines Brochure Printing Cost in Kampala

1) Quantity

Unit cost reduces as quantity increases. For campaigns, higher quantities usually offer better value per brochure.

2) Paper Weight & Type

Heavier paper costs more but improves feel and durability. Gloss/matte art paper is common for rich color brochures.

3) Fold Complexity

Tri-fold and gate-fold have more finishing steps than bi-fold. They also require accurate panel alignment and safe margins.

4) Lamination & Creasing

Lamination improves durability. Creasing prevents cracking on thicker paper and heavy ink coverage.

5) Artwork Readiness

Print-ready files reduce delays. If the file needs corrections (safe margins, folding alignment, image resolution), this affects turnaround. If you need layout support, see Graphic Design.

6) Turnaround & Delivery

Urgent jobs may require priority scheduling. Delivery location and packaging needs can also affect planning.

Brochure Printing Prices Kampala — FAQs

How is brochure printing priced in Kampala?

Brochure pricing is typically based on final folded size, paper type/weight (GSM), whether you need full color on one side or both sides, quantity, and fold type. Optional finishing (lamination and creasing) is added if required. For technical details on formats, see Brochure Printing Kampala.

What brochure type is most common for Kampala businesses?

Tri-fold brochures are common because they provide multiple panels in a compact DL-style format and work well for services, menus, price lists and corporate promotions. If you’re running a wider campaign, pair brochures with Flyer Printing for distribution.

Do you recommend lamination for brochures?

For brochures that will be handled frequently or used for corporate presentations, lamination improves durability and makes colors more stable over time. Matte lamination is common for a corporate look. For premium finishing options, see Print Finishing Services.

Why do brochures crack on folds?

Cracking can happen with thicker paper, heavy ink coverage, or dark solid areas crossing the fold. Creasing (scoring) before folding reduces cracking and improves accuracy, especially on premium brochures. If your layout needs fold-safe adjustments, our Graphic Design team can help.

Can you help with brochure design if I don’t have artwork?

Yes. We design brochure layouts and prepare print-ready, fold-safe files (margins, bleed, panel widths and correct export settings). Start via Contact or explore Graphic Design Kampala.

How do I send you brochure specs for a quote?

Share brochure type (bi-fold/tri-fold), final folded size, quantity, paper preference (or ask us to recommend), finishing (lamination/creasing), and deadline. Use Contact or WhatsApp. If you are planning multiple print items, see Printing Pricing.

Do you deliver brochures in Kampala and nearby areas?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and surrounding areas. Share your delivery location and deadline early so we plan packaging and logistics. For event deadlines, also see Event Branding.

Can you support procurement requirements for institutions?

Yes. We provide structured quotations with clear scope and can align to your approval process. For documentation, see Corporate Profile and Compliance.
