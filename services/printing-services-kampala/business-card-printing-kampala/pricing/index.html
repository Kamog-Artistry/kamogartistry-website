Finishing Options (What Changes the Price)

Finishing affects both cost and how the card feels in the hand. For a strong first impression, finishing should match brand positioning: corporate, premium, minimalist, luxury, or mass distribution.

Matte Lamination

Professional soft finish; reduces glare; works well with spot UV highlights.

Gloss Lamination

Bright shine and color pop; popular for vibrant designs and promotional cards.

Spot UV / Raised UV

Adds a glossy raised effect on selected areas (logo/name); requires correct artwork layer setup.

Foil Stamping

Premium metallic finish (gold/silver/colored foil). Best for luxury and executive branding.

Emboss / Deboss

Creates a tactile raised/pressed impression. Great for clean logos and minimal designs.

Rounded Corners / Die-Cut

Modern look or unique shapes. Pricing depends on cut complexity and quantity.

What Determines Business Card Printing Cost in Kampala

1) Quantity

Unit price often reduces with higher quantities. Tell us your target quantity and we’ll recommend the most cost-effective run.

2) Paper Thickness (Card Stock)

Thicker stocks feel premium and hold up better. Thickness choice is one of the biggest pricing factors after finishing.

3) Printing Sides & Color Coverage

Double-sided printing and heavy ink coverage can affect production planning. Share whether you want a white margin or full bleed.

4) Finishing & Special Effects

Lamination is the common upgrade. Spot UV, foil and embossing require extra processes and setup, which increases cost. For advanced finishes, see Print Finishing Services.

5) Multiple Names (Staff Card Sets)

Corporate orders may include many names and job titles. We quote based on quantity per person and file management needs.

6) Turnaround & Delivery

Urgent jobs may require priority scheduling. Share your deadline and delivery location for accurate planning.

Business Card Printing Prices Kampala — FAQs

How much does it cost to print 100 business cards in Kampala?

For standard cards, budgeting often starts from the lower bracket and increases based on thickness, size, and whether printing is single or double-sided. If you add lamination, spot UV, foil, embossing, rounded corners or die-cutting, the price increases due to extra finishing steps. For the fastest accurate quote, use the quote checklist and contact us via Contact or WhatsApp.

What is the difference between standard and premium business cards?

Premium cards typically use thicker stock and can include finishes like matte lamination, spot UV, foiling or embossing. They feel more durable and improve brand perception in corporate settings. For options and recommended specs, see Business Card Printing Kampala.

Does lamination increase business card price?

Yes. Lamination adds a finishing step, but it improves durability and appearance. Matte lamination is popular for a professional look, while gloss lamination makes colors look brighter. If you’re comparing finishes, see Print Finishing Services.

Do you print business cards for multiple staff names?

Yes. Corporate staff card sets can be quoted based on quantity per person, number of names, and finishing type. If you have many staff names, send the list early (name + title + contact details) so we standardize formatting and schedule production.

What file format should I send for business cards?

Print-ready PDF is preferred. If you have an editable source (AI/PSD/INDD), we can work with it where needed. If you’re not sure, upload artwork for a quick check (bleed, safe margin, font clarity and output setup), or review our Print-Ready Artwork guide.

Can you design my business card before printing?

Yes. We can design a clean layout that fits your brand identity and finishing choice, then print it. Start via Contact or explore Branding Kampala.

How fast can you print business cards in Kampala?

Turnaround depends on quantity, finishing and artwork readiness. Standard cards are usually faster than specialty finishes like foil or embossing. If you have a deadline, share it early so we confirm scheduling.

Do you deliver business cards around Kampala?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and nearby areas. Share delivery location and timing when requesting a quote.
