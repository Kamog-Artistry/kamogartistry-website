Our Business Card Printing Process

We keep it simple: confirm details, check artwork, proof, then print and finish. This prevents spelling mistakes, misalignment, weak finishing, and reprint inconsistencies.

1) Confirm Details

Quantity, size, single/double sided, paper type, finishing, and deadline. We advise what fits your use-case and budget.

2) Artwork Check

We check spelling, margins, safe areas, and print setup. If needed, we correct spacing and typography for readability.

3) Proof Approval

You approve the proof before we print—this protects your budget and avoids “wrong number / wrong title” mistakes.

4) Print, Finish & Delivery

We print, cut cleanly, finish as selected (lamination/spot UV/etc.) and package properly for delivery or pickup.

Turnaround guidance

Turnaround depends on quantity and finishing (standard matte/gloss is typically faster; spot UV/emboss may add steps). If you have an event deadline, also explore Banner Printing and Poster Printing for onsite visibility.

Common Business Card Mistakes We Prevent

Most “bad business cards” are caused by layout and setup errors. We fix these before printing so you don’t waste money.

Too Small Text

Contacts become unreadable, especially on dark backgrounds or low-contrast layouts.

Bad Margins

Text too close to edges gets trimmed or looks cramped and unprofessional.

Low-Quality Logo

Pixelated logos print poorly—vector logos are clean, sharp and premium.

No Proof Approval

Wrong phone number, wrong title or spelling errors become expensive once printing starts.

Wrong Finish Choice

Some designs look better in matte; others need soft-touch or selective spot UV. We guide you.

Overcrowded Layout

Too many icons and lines reduce clarity. We simplify for professionalism and readability.

What do you need to quote business card printing?

Which finish looks most professional?

Matte is a clean corporate standard. Soft-touch feels premium for executives. Spot UV works best when used selectively on logos or key details. For finishing options across multiple print items, see Print Finishing Services.
