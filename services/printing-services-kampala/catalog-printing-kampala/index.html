Catalog Binding Options (Choose Based on Page Count & Use)

Binding affects durability, usability and perceived quality. We recommend binding based on page count, thickness, and how often the catalog will be handled.

Saddle Stitch (Stapled)

Best for slimmer catalogs and promotional booklets. It opens flat and is cost-effective.

  • Ideal for: 8–48 pages (depending on paper thickness)
  • Page count must be a multiple of 4 (e.g., 8, 12, 16, 20, 24...)
  • Great for: mini-catalogs, product guides, exhibition handouts, seasonal promos

Perfect Binding (Softcover Spine)

Best for thicker catalogs that must look premium and professional—clean spine for labeling and shelf storage.

  • Ideal for: full product catalogues, price books, company catalogues and academic publications
  • Options: EVA or stronger PUR glue (durability-focused)
  • Great for: corporate catalogs, dealer catalogs, exhibition books and annual programme guides

Wire-O / Spiral Binding

Practical for catalogs that must open fully and stay open—useful for staff, training, menus and technical manuals.

  • Ideal for: reference catalogs, manuals and academic desk use
  • Stays open for easy browsing
  • Durable for repeated daily use

Common sizes + what they’re best for

  • A4: corporate catalogues, procurement packs, exhibition catalogues, academic guides and product ranges with specs and tables.
  • A5: pocket catalogues, mini-catalogs, promos and fast distribution.
  • Square / custom: lookbooks, premium brand presentations and showroom catalogs.
Choosing between PUR and EVA?
If your catalog will be handled a lot or must last longer, ask for a stronger perfect binding option.

Common Catalog Mistakes We Fix (Before Printing)

Catalogs can look cheap fast when structure and finishing are ignored. Here are the key issues we prevent.

Wrong Binding

Using saddle stitch for a thick catalogue, or choosing weak binding for heavy daily handling.

Bad Page Count

Saddle stitch requires page counts in multiples of 4—otherwise content must be adjusted.

Weak Hierarchy

No categories, no index, unclear specs or section flow—readers get confused and stop reading.

Cheap Feel

Thin covers and no protective finish leads to scuffs, bends, and poor first impression.

Low-Quality Images

Pixelated product photos or artwork reduce trust. We flag issues early so your print looks sharp.

Tables Not Readable

Pricing tables, programme structures or listings that are too small become unusable. We guide typography and spacing.

Catalog Printing Kampala — FAQs

What is the best binding for a catalog?

For slimmer catalogues and promotional booklets, saddle stitch is common. For thicker catalogs that must look premium and durable, perfect binding is recommended. For reference use that must stay open on a desk, wire-o/spiral is practical. If you’re unsure, share your page count via Contact.

Does a saddle-stitched catalog need special page counts?

Yes. Because it’s made from folded sheets, the final page count must be a multiple of 4 (e.g., 8, 12, 16, 20, 24...). For thicker catalogs, consider perfect binding.

Can you print a catalog with a spine title?

Yes—perfect bound catalogs have a clean spine where the title can be placed (depending on thickness). This is ideal for company catalogues, exhibition books and academic publications.

What finishing makes a catalog look premium?

Matte or gloss lamination protects the cover. For premium brands, spot UV, foil or emboss/deboss can elevate presentation. If you want finishing-only work, see Print Finishing Services.

Can you print exhibition catalogues or academic catalogues?

Yes. We print exhibition catalogues, programme guides, prospectuses, academic catalogues, institutional reference books and other structured publications depending on page count and binding needs.

Can you design the catalog too?

Yes. We can structure categories, prepare product layouts, and create print-ready files. See Graphic Design Kampala.

What do you need to quote catalog printing?

Share size, page count, quantity, binding choice, finishing needs, content status (ready/draft), and deadline. You can also review quote guidance on Printing Pricing.

Do you deliver catalogs in Kampala?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and nearby areas. Share your location and deadline so we plan delivery. For corporate workflows, you can reference our Corporate Profile.
