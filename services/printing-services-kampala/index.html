Commercial Printing Capabilities

Choose the right production path for your quantity, turnaround and finish. These pages make the selection easier: Digital Printing and Offset Printing — then complete the job with Print Finishing Services.

Digital Printing

Best for short-to-mid runs, urgent timelines and quick marketing materials — ideal for flyers, brochures, posters and fast corporate documents.

Offset Printing

Best for bulk campaigns and high volume — lower unit cost and strong consistency for catalogs, magazines, books and repeat corporate jobs.

Print Finishing

Finishing improves durability and presentation: lamination, folding, binding, trimming and production checks that protect brand quality.

Paper & Material Options

Material choice affects durability, colour perception and overall presentation. We recommend what fits your use-case and budget.

  • Art Paper: 130gsm, 170gsm, 250gsm, 300gsm for brochures, covers and premium marketing.
  • Bond Paper: 80gsm, 100gsm for internal documents and office print.
  • Cover Stocks / Boards: for durability and premium feel on booklets, catalogs and covers.
  • Lamination: gloss and matt for protection and premium presentation.
  • Finishing: folding, binding, trimming and production checks.
  • Banner Media: PVC options for outdoor promotion and durable campaigns.
  • Sticker Media: adhesive materials depending on use-case and surface.

Printing Services Kampala — FAQs

What printing services do you offer in Kampala?

We print business cards, flyers, brochures, posters, banners, stickers, catalogs, books, magazines, calendars and corporate documents. We also handle digital printing, offset printing and finishing based on quantity, deadline and presentation needs.

What information do you need to quote a print job?

Share the item type, size, quantity, number of pages where relevant, paper preference if known, finishing requirements, artwork status and deadline. These details make the quotation faster and more accurate.

Do you review artwork before printing?

Yes. We check bleed, margins, resolution and layout safety so trimming, folding and binding do not cut important content. You can upload artwork for review before production.

What is the difference between digital and offset printing?

Digital printing is best for quick turnaround, short-to-mid runs and urgent marketing materials. Offset printing is best for high-volume jobs, repeat corporate materials and consistent colour across larger quantities.

Do you print business cards, flyers and brochures?

Yes. We print business cards, flyers and brochures for companies, SMEs, events, NGOs, schools and retail promotions. Start with business cards, flyers or brochures.

Do you print banners and large format materials?

Yes. We print banners and large format materials for promotions, launches, shop fronts, events and campaigns. See Banner Printing and Large Format Printing.

Do you print books, catalogs and magazines?

Yes. Multi-page print jobs can include layout checks, paper recommendations, binding guidance and finishing. See Book Printing, Catalog Printing and Magazine Printing.

What paper and finishing options are available?

Common options include bond paper, art paper, cover stocks, matt or gloss lamination, folding, trimming and binding. The best choice depends on the product, budget, durability and how the material will be used.

Can you print urgent jobs in Kampala?

Urgent jobs depend on quantity, artwork readiness, print method and finishing. Send complete specs and the deadline through WhatsApp or the contact page so the fastest practical production route can be advised.

Can you design the artwork before printing?

Yes. If artwork is not ready, request design or print file preparation before production. This is useful for flyers, brochures, business cards, posters, catalogs and campaign materials.

Do you work with SMEs, NGOs, schools and corporates?

Yes. We print for SMEs, NGOs, schools, institutions, clinics, retail businesses, events and corporate teams across Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and wider Uganda.

How do we start a printing job?

Send your specs, deadline and artwork through Contact or WhatsApp. Include size, quantity, paper preference if known and any finishing requirements.
