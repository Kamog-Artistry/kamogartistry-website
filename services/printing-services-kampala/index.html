Our Printing Process

1) Specs & Recommendation

We confirm size, quantity and deadline, then recommend paper, method (digital/offset) and finishing.

2) Artwork Check

We verify bleed, margins, resolution and layout safety so trimming, folding and binding stay clean.

3) Production + Finishing

Printing and finishing based on your job: trimming, folding, lamination, binding or other requirements.

4) Pickup / Delivery

Pickup in Kampala or delivery coordination—especially for bulk and campaign jobs.

Paper & Material Options

Material choice affects durability, color perception and overall presentation. If you’re unsure, we recommend what fits your use-case and budget.

  • Art Paper: 130gsm, 170gsm, 250gsm, 300gsm (common for brochures, covers and premium marketing)
  • Bond Paper: 80gsm, 100gsm (common for internal documents and office print)
  • Cover Stocks / Boards: for durability and premium feel on booklets, catalogs and covers
  • Lamination: gloss and matt for protection and premium presentation
  • Finishing: folding, binding, trimming and production checks
  • Banner Media: PVC options for outdoor promotion and durable campaigns
  • Sticker Media: adhesive materials depending on use-case and surface

For production steps and finishing types, see Print Finishing Services Kampala.

Printing Services Kampala — FAQs

What information do you need to quote a print job?

Share the size, quantity, item type (cards, flyers, brochure, poster, catalog, book, magazine), and your deadline. If you’re unsure about method, start with Digital Printing or Offset Printing and we’ll recommend the best route.

Do you help check artwork before printing?

Yes. We check bleed, margins, resolution and layout safety so trimming and folding don’t cut important content. You can upload artwork for review before production.

What paper and finishing options are available?

Paper and finishing depend on the product and use-case. For professional finishing options (lamination, folding, binding and trimming), see Print Finishing Services.

What’s the difference between digital and offset printing?

Digital printing is best for quick turnaround and short-to-mid runs. Offset is best for high volume and consistency on repeat jobs. Compare both here: Digital Printing vs Offset Printing.

Do you print banners and large format materials?

Yes. For banners, start with Banner Printing. For oversized visibility prints, see Large Format Printing.

What’s the typical turnaround time for printing?

Turnaround depends on quantity, method and finishing—plus how quickly artwork is approved. For urgent jobs, WhatsApp your specs and deadline, or start on Contact so we can advise the fastest production path.
