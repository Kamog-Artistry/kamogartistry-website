Sticker Types Commonly Printed in Uganda (What Each Is Best For)

Below are the most practical sticker types used by Kampala businesses, institutions, NGOs and retail brands. If you’re not sure which fits, we’ll recommend based on application and lifespan.

Vinyl Stickers (Most Popular)

  • Works well with lamination (gloss or matte)
  • Great for outdoor exposure and handling

Product Labels (Packaging Stickers)

Labels for bottles, jars, boxes, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, food packaging and FMCG. We guide on finish so labels stay clean and readable.

Clear / Transparent Stickers

Clean “no-background” look on glass, bottles and premium packaging. Perfect for minimal, high-end branding.

One Way Vision (Perforated Window Film)

Perforated film for glass windows and vehicle rear windows—full-color branding outside while maintaining visibility from inside.

Frosted Film Stickers (Glass Privacy)

Materials, Lamination & Finishes (How to Choose Like a Pro)

Most sticker “problems” come from the wrong finish: ink scratches, water damage, peeling edges, or fading. Here is how we select the best setup.

Gloss Lamination

Bright, high contrast and “pop”—great for promotions, product visuals, and colorful branding.

  • Vibrant look
  • Good protection against scratches
  • Easy wipe-clean surface

Matte Lamination

Premium “executive” look with reduced glare—best for corporate labels, office environments and classy packaging.

  • Less reflection
  • Premium feel
  • Great for text-heavy labels

Waterproof & Outdoor Durability

For outdoor use, frequent handling, and water exposure, vinyl + lamination is the safe baseline.

  • Recommended for vehicles & outdoor branding
  • Better resistance to wear
  • Cleaner long-term look

Adhesive Choice

Permanent vs removable adhesive matters. We guide based on whether you need long-term branding or temporary promotions.

  • Permanent (long-term branding)
  • Removable (campaigns, temporary labels)
  • Surface-specific guidance (glass, plastic, metal)

Practical Kampala note

For window branding, we recommend either one-way vision for see-through advertising or frosted film for privacy. For night visibility, we recommend reflective (standard or honeycomb/microprismatic).

Cut Styles: Die-Cut, Kiss-Cut & Sheet Stickers

Cutting changes how your stickers look, peel, and feel. We’ll recommend based on your budget and usage.

Die-Cut (Individual Shape)

Each sticker is cut to the exact shape. Best for premium branding, giveaways, product packs and clean presentation.

  • Premium look
  • Great for logos and custom shapes
  • Perfect for promos and giveaways

Kiss-Cut (Easy Peel)

The sticker is cut but stays on a backing sheet. This makes peeling easy and protects the edges during handling.

  • Easy peel for staff usage
  • Good for product labeling workflows
  • Clean storage and transport

Sheet Stickers (Multiple Per Sheet)

Multiple stickers arranged on one sheet. Great for bulk labels, packaging workflows, and cost-efficient production.

  • Cost-efficient for bulk
  • Good for repeated labeling
  • Easy distribution to teams
Not sure which cut you need?
If it’s for giveaways or premium branding: die-cut. For staff labeling: kiss-cut or sheets.

Application & Surface Preparation Tips (Avoid Peeling)

Even the best sticker can fail if applied on a dirty or textured surface. These quick steps help your stickers last longer—especially on vehicles, glass, and packaging.

Clean the Surface

Remove dust, oil, and wax. Glass and painted surfaces should be clean and dry before application.

  • Use a clean cloth
  • Avoid applying on oily/greasy surfaces
  • Dry fully before sticking

Apply With Firm Pressure

Press from the center outward to reduce bubbles. For large films, we recommend professional installation for a clean finish.

  • Center-out smoothing
  • Reduce bubbles and edge lift
  • Installation support available

Wait Before Washing

For vehicle decals and outdoor stickers, allow time for adhesion to bond before washing or heavy exposure.

  • Let adhesive set properly
  • Better long-term hold
  • Less edge peeling

Edges Matter

Rounded corners and lamination improve durability on high-touch areas like bottles and delivery boxes.

  • Rounded corners last longer
  • Lamination helps against scratches
  • Better for frequent handling

Sticker Printing Kampala — FAQs

What is the best sticker type for bottles and product packaging?

For packaging, vinyl-based labels with the right finish are most reliable. If you want a premium minimal look, clear stickers are great on glass and smooth bottles. Share your container type and we’ll recommend the best option.

Are vinyl stickers waterproof?

Vinyl stickers are generally more durable than paper stickers. For water exposure and long-term handling, we recommend vinyl plus lamination for extra protection.

What is one-way vision sticker material?

One-way vision is a perforated window film used on glass and vehicle rear windows. Graphics show on the outside while you can still see from inside—useful for advertising without blocking visibility.

What are reflective stickers used for?

Reflective stickers increase visibility under headlights and low light. They’re common for safety marking on vehicles, helmets, security equipment, delivery boxes and warning labels. Honeycomb/microprismatic reflective options give a stronger reflective effect.

What is frosted film sticker and where is it used?

Frosted film is used on glass partitions to create privacy while still allowing light. It’s popular for offices, clinics, banks, salons, meeting rooms and reception areas.

What is the difference between die-cut and kiss-cut stickers?

Die-cut stickers are individual stickers cut to shape. Kiss-cut stickers are cut but remain on a backing sheet, making them easier to peel and handle—great for staff labeling workflows.

What files do you need for printing stickers?

Provide the exact size and a high-quality file (PDF preferred). If you need die-cut or kiss-cut, we also need a cut shape/outline—if you don’t have it, we can create it.

How do we start a sticker printing order?

Share sticker type, size, quantity, finishing (gloss/matte lamination) and where it will be applied. Start via Contact or WhatsApp us for quick guidance.
