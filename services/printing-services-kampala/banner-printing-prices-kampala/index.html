Finishing Add-ons That Affect Banner Pricing

Many pricing disputes happen because finishing is not specified. A banner can be printed at a low base price and fail quickly if it is not finished properly. Below are the most requested finishing options in Kampala and what they change in the quote.

Hemming (Reinforced Edges)

Reinforces edges to reduce tearing and improves durability, especially outdoors and for larger banners.

Eyelets / Grommets

Adds strong mounting points. Eyelet spacing depends on size, wind exposure, and how you intend to install (wall, fence, poles).

Pockets / Sleeves

Creates a sleeve for poles or frames for a cleaner, straighter hang. Common for stages, backdrops, and indoor setups.

Seaming (Joining Panels)

For extra-large banners, multiple panels may be joined. This affects finishing time and how the banner is installed.

Stands (Roll-Up / X-Stand)

Roll-up and X-stand banners are priced per unit because the stand/base is included. The print media must also match the stand system.

Installation Planning

Delivery location, mounting height, and site conditions can affect logistics planning. For long-term installations, consider signage fabrication.

What Determines Banner Printing Cost in Kampala

1) Material Selection

PVC is the common baseline. Mesh is better in wind. Backlit is for illumination. Blockout reduces show-through. Fabric options are commonly paired with poles for activations.

2) Size (Square Meter Calculation)

Most printed banners are priced per square meter. Accurate width × height avoids surprises. For multiple banners, the quote is based on total output plus finishing.

3) Finishing & Mounting Requirements

Hemming, eyelets, sleeves, seaming, and stand systems add steps and cost—often worth it for durability and professional presentation.

4) Artwork Readiness

Low resolution or incorrect setup creates rework. We check margins, safe zones, and readability, especially for roadside banners and large backdrops.

5) Turnaround & Scheduling

Urgent jobs may require priority scheduling. Clear approvals and a ready artwork file help keep timelines predictable.

6) Delivery / Handling

Large quantities, oversized banners, and event deadlines may require delivery coordination. Share the location and install time early.

If you are planning a full campaign, combine banners with: Posters, Flyers, Brochures, and Stickers for consistent visibility.

Banner Printing Prices Kampala — FAQs

Do you charge banners per square meter in Kampala?

Most standard banners (PVC flex, mesh, backlit, blockout) are priced per square meter based on width × height, then finishing (hemming/eyelets/sleeves) is added based on requirements.

Why do two suppliers quote different prices for the same banner?

Differences usually come from the banner media type/weight, print method, and finishing quality (hemming, eyelets, seam strength). Also confirm whether delivery or stands are included.

What is the best banner type for windy areas?

Mesh banners are commonly recommended for fences and open compounds because they allow airflow and reduce tearing under wind load.

Do roll-up (pull-up) banners include the stand in the price?

Yes. Roll-up banner pricing is typically per unit and includes the base, support pole, and often a carry bag. Size and stand quality affect pricing.

What finishing should I request for outdoor PVC flex banners?

Outdoor PVC banners typically require hemming and eyelets for secure mounting. Eyelet spacing should match the size and the mounting location.

Can you check my artwork before printing to avoid blurred banners?

Yes. We check resolution, safe margins, and readability (especially for roadside visibility) before production. You can upload artwork for review.

Do you deliver banners in Kampala and nearby areas?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and surrounding areas. Share your location and deadline so we plan delivery and handover.

How do I get an official quotation for procurement?

Send your banner specs, quantities and delivery location. If you have an approval process or documentation requirements, share them early so the quote scope matches your procurement workflow.
