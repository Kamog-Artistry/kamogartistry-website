Poster Sizes We Print (A0–A4 + Custom)

Use standard sizes for easy framing and predictable costs. We also print custom sizes when your space or campaign requires it.

Size Dimensions (mm) Best For Common Placement
A0 841 × 1189 High-impact visibility Events, exhibitions, large indoor boards
A1 594 × 841 Promotions and announcements Walls, shop displays, campus noticeboards

Size selection tip (simple rule)

If you want the message to be read from further away, go bigger (A1/A0). If it’s for close viewing like an office noticeboard, A3/A2 usually works best.

Indoor vs Outdoor Posters (Choose the Right Option)

Indoor and outdoor posters are not the same. Indoor posters are optimized for appearance and display comfort. Outdoor posters must handle humidity, sunlight, and exposure—so material choice matters.

Indoor Posters

Best for offices, shops, reception areas, exhibitions and noticeboards. Designed for clean look and crisp color.

  • Best for protected indoor spaces
  • Great for sharp detail and premium look
  • Optional lamination for durability

Outdoor Posters

Best for street campaigns, outdoor panels, and areas that can get damp or exposed. Material and finish affect lifespan.

  • Better resistance to exposure
  • Good for short-term campaigns
  • Ask for blueback when pasting over older posters

Window Posters

Great for shops, salons, restaurants and promotions. We advise on glare control and readability through glass.

  • Promotions and price posters
  • Good for seasonal campaigns
  • Readable from outside

Campaign Posting

If posters will be pasted on public boards or over existing posters, choose options designed to reduce show-through and support fast rollout.

  • Bulk-friendly
  • Street distribution use
  • High visibility designs

Artwork Specs for Poster Printing (Avoid White Borders & Blurry Output)

Posters fail when artwork is not set up correctly. We check your files before printing to reduce the most common issues: wrong size, low resolution images, missing bleed, and important text too close to the edge.

Bleed (Recommended for Full-Edge Posters)

If your design prints to the edge, include bleed so trimming doesn’t leave thin white lines around the poster.

  • Use bleed for full background designs
  • Keep bleed area as background only
  • Ask us if you’re exporting from Canva

Safe Margins

Keep important text, logos and QR codes away from the edge so trimming doesn’t cut or crowd your content.

  • Headline away from the trim
  • Keep contacts readable
  • QR codes need breathing space

Resolution & Images

Large posters need clean images. Avoid screenshots and low-quality downloads—these become blurry when enlarged.

  • Use high-quality photos
  • Avoid pixelated logos
  • Provide vector logos when possible

Best File Format

PDF is preferred for reliable printing. If you have editable files, share them too for last-minute corrections.

  • PDF (print-ready)
  • Editable: AI / PSD / INDD (optional)
  • Confirm final size before export

Poster Printing Kampala — FAQs

Do you print political campaign posters in Kampala?

Yes. We print political campaign posters in bulk for distribution and posting, and we can recommend the best material (including blueback) based on where the posters will be placed. See Political Campaign Posters.

Which material is best for campaign posters pasted over older posters?

Blueback poster paper is commonly recommended because it helps reduce show-through from older posters, improving visibility during street posting.

What poster sizes do you print in Kampala?

We print A0, A1, A2, A3 and A4 posters, plus custom sizes depending on your placement and campaign needs.

What’s the difference between indoor and outdoor posters?

Indoor posters focus on appearance and are best for protected spaces. Outdoor posters need better resistance for exposure and are recommended for street posting and short-term outdoor campaigns.

What is blueback poster paper and when do I need it?

Blueback is designed for outdoor advertising where posters may be pasted over older posters. The blue backing helps reduce show-through so the message remains clear.

Do I need bleed on my poster artwork?

If your design has background color or images going to the edge, bleed is recommended to prevent white borders after trimming. We can advise based on your file.

Can you design the poster too?

Yes. We can design and prepare a print-ready file, then print and deliver. See Graphic Design Kampala.

What do you need to quote poster printing?

Yes, depending on size, quantity and finishing. WhatsApp your file and deadline and we’ll confirm the fastest safe timeline.
