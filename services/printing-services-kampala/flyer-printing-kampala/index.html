Flyer Sizes We Print (A6, A5, A4, DL & Custom)

Size affects cost, readability, and distribution speed. The best flyer size depends on how much you need to say and where you will distribute it. Below are popular choices for Kampala campaigns.

A6 Flyers (Handouts)

Compact and cost-effective for mass distribution. Best for simple offers, quick service lists, and short event details.

  • Easy to hand out quickly
  • Best for short messaging
  • Great for street campaigns

A5 Flyers (Most Popular)

Balanced space and cost. Works well for product photos + details, service lists, promotions and event flyers.

  • Good space for visuals
  • Readable without crowding
  • Excellent for counters & handouts

A4 Flyers (Detailed)

More room for schedules, menus, product lists, and detailed information. Best when you need clarity and structure.

  • Best for lots of info
  • Works for price lists & menus
  • Great for office distribution

DL Flyers (Slim & Premium)

Ideal for rack displays, inserts, and neat premium presentation. Great for salons, restaurants, and service menus.

  • Nice for envelopes & inserts
  • Good for menu-style layouts
  • Looks premium with matte finish

Paper Options & GSM (Budget vs Premium Flyers)

Paper weight (GSM) changes how your flyer feels, how long it lasts, and how “premium” your brand looks. We help you match paper choice to purpose—mass distribution or high-end presentation.

Budget Flyers (Mass Distribution)

Best when you are targeting large numbers: door drops, campus distribution, street handouts, and fast promotions.

  • Light to medium stock
  • Cost-effective per piece
  • Good for short campaign periods

Professional & Premium Flyers

Best when your brand needs credibility: corporate offerings, premium products, real estate, clinics, and high-value services.

  • Medium to heavy stock
  • Better “hand feel” and durability
  • Works well with matte or gloss lamination

Gloss / Satin Paper

Makes colors pop—great for product photos, restaurants, promotions, and bold campaigns.

Matte Paper

Premium and modern—great for corporate, professional services, and clean brand presentation.

Cardstock (Thicker)

Best for premium handouts and mini-brochure feel. Stronger durability for repeated handling.

Double-Sided Printing

Use both sides for better ROI: front for attention, back for details, location, terms, and contact.

Flyer Design Tips That Increase Response

Most flyers fail because the message is unclear. A strong flyer is structured: headline → offer → proof → location → call-to-action. If you want higher response rates in Kampala, use the checklist below.

One Clear Goal

Don’t sell 10 things on one flyer. Pick one offer or one message and make it strong.

Big Headline

Your headline must be readable from a quick glance—avoid small text blocks.

Proof & Trust

Add real proof: years, location, key clients, warranty, delivery, or guarantees.

Strong CTA

Use WhatsApp, phone, and a simple location direction—make action easy.

High-performing CTA examples

  • WhatsApp now for price + availability
  • Visit today (include landmark + area)
  • Call to book (clinic, salon, service)
  • Scan QR to see catalog / offers / Google map

Artwork Specs (Print-Ready Flyers)

Most reprints happen due to file mistakes: missing bleed, tiny margins, blurry images, or wrong export settings. We check your file before printing and advise corrections if needed.

Bleed for Full-Color Edges

If your design has background color/images to the edge, add bleed so trimming doesn’t leave white lines.

Safe Margins

Keep key text/logos away from the edges so they don’t get cut during finishing.

Sharp Images

Low-quality images print poorly. We help you avoid pixelation and weak visuals.

Preferred File Type

PDF is preferred for consistent output. If you have editable files (AI/PSD), send them too.

Flyer Printing Kampala — FAQs

What is the best flyer size in Kampala?

A5 is the most popular because it balances space and cost. A6 is best for mass handouts, A4 is best for detailed information, and DL is great for slim menu-style layouts and inserts.

Should I print single-sided or double-sided?

Double-sided gives better ROI: use the front for attention and the back for details, location, terms, QR code and contacts—without overcrowding.

What paper is best for flyers?

Budget flyers use lighter paper for mass distribution. Professional flyers use medium paper for better feel. Premium flyers use thicker paper and often lamination for stronger durability and credibility.

Can you help with flyer design?

Yes. We can design your flyer layout, structure the message, and prepare print-ready files. If you have content already, we can refine and format it for better response.

What do you need to quote flyer printing?

Can you print urgent flyers?

Yes, depending on quantity and finishing. WhatsApp your design and deadline and we’ll confirm the fastest safe timeline.
