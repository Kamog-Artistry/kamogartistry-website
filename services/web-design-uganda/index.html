Our Website Development Process

This process reflects how serious website projects should move: approved direction first, then discovery, architecture, content, design, development, review, deployment and growth.

1) Proposal & Scope Approval

We begin with an approved direction, website objectives, deliverables, budget and expected project outcome.

2) Discovery & Document Review

We review company profiles, service information, project records, photos, certificates, reports and available business documents.

3) Website Architecture Planning

We map pages, service hubs, portfolio areas, content sections, navigation and internal links before full build.

4) Content Collection & Authority Mapping

We identify missing content, verify project information, collect supporting evidence and map services to relevant pages.

5) Design & Development

We create the visual layout, responsive structure, content flow and technical build using a clean performance-focused approach.

6) SEO & Digital Authority Setup

We prepare metadata, schema, internal linking, sitemap structure, search-friendly headings and authority content foundations.

7) Review, Refinement & Approval

The client reviews the website, provides corrections, confirms content accuracy and approves final adjustments.

8) Deployment & Post-Launch Support

We deploy the website, confirm links and forms, check indexing basics and advise on future updates and growth.

Website Pricing in Uganda — What Affects Cost?

Number of Pages

A 5-page website costs less than a service authority platform with many service pages, project pages and resources.

Content Readiness

If content is ready, development moves faster. If we must collect, structure and write content, the scope increases.

Portfolio & Case Studies

Project documentation, galleries, case studies and proof systems require more planning but increase credibility.

SEO & Authority Depth

Metadata, schema, internal linking, service hubs, sitemaps and authority pages add strategic value beyond basic design.

Technical Setup

Domain setup, hosting, business emails, redirects, analytics, forms and deployment requirements affect the final quotation.

Maintenance Needs

Ongoing updates, new pages, project uploads, blog posts and technical checks can be quoted as monthly or on-demand support.
