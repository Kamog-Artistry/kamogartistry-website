What Affects Custom Merchandise Prices?

Merchandise pricing is not one flat number because every order combines product quality, branding method, quantity, packaging and delivery requirements. A branded pen for a conference has different cost drivers from an embroidered cap, corporate gift set, trophy or branded water bottle.

Main Cost Drivers

1) Item Type & Quality

A basic pen, premium notebook, cotton t-shirt, cap, umbrella, water bottle or trophy all carry different base costs and quality options.

2) Quantity

Large quantities can reduce unit cost because setup time and production handling are spread across more pieces.

3) Branding Method

Screen print, heat transfer, embroidery, engraving, sticker branding and UV print each suit different products and budgets.

4) Branding Positions

One logo placement is usually simpler than multiple positions, sleeves, backs, packaging labels or personalised names.

5) Artwork Readiness

Clean vector artwork speeds up production. Low-resolution logos may require redrawing or artwork correction before branding.

6) Packaging & Delivery

Gift boxes, inserts, wrapping, sorting by recipient and delivery coordination can change the final quote.

Custom Merchandise Pricing — FAQs

Why are merchandise prices quoted instead of fixed?

Prices change based on item quality, quantity, supplier availability, branding method, packaging and delivery needs. A tailored quote gives a more accurate total for your exact order.

What is the fastest way to get a quote?

Send the item list, quantities, logo files, deadline and delivery location. Reference photos are useful when you want a specific style or quality level.

Does bulk quantity reduce unit cost?

Often yes. Bulk orders can reduce unit cost because setup and production handling are spread across more items.

Do you charge separately for artwork setup?

It depends on the condition of your files. Clean vector artwork is easier to prepare, while low-resolution logos may require redrawing or layout correction.

Can you price a full corporate gift package?

Yes. Share your budget range, recipient type, number of packs and deadline. We can recommend item combinations and packaging options.

Can printing be bundled with merchandise?

Yes. Printed inserts, flyers, cards, brochures and event materials can be coordinated through Printing Services Kampala.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes. Delivery can be coordinated across Uganda depending on order size, deadline and location.

How do we approve before production?

You review mockups showing placement, size and colours before production starts.
