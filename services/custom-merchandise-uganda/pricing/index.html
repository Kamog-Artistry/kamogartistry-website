What Affects Merchandise Cost in Kampala

1) Item Quality & Grade

Standard vs premium items (materials, durability, brand grade) affect cost and perception.

2) Branding Method

UV print, screen print, engraving, embroidery and sublimation each have different setup and durability.

3) Branding Positions & Coverage

One-side branding costs less than two-sides or wrap branding. Large coverage increases time/material.

4) Quantity

Bulk orders reduce unit cost. Mixing many variations may affect batching and timelines.

5) Packaging

Individual boxes, gift boxes, inserts and thank-you cards increase the finished feel and cost.

6) Timeline & Delivery

Rush deadlines may require priority scheduling. Delivery depends on location and package volume.

Custom Merchandise Uganda Pricing — FAQs

What do you need to quote merchandise accurately?

Item type, quantity, branding method, branding positions, item grade, packaging preference and deadline. Use the Fast Quote Checklist.

Do prices include design?

Basic logo placement is usually straightforward. Full layout design, custom illustrations or complex wrap designs are quoted based on complexity.

Can you match a competitor sample?

Yes—share clear photos and we’ll match the look and propose a more cost-effective option where possible (item grade and branding method considered).

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes. Bulk quantities reduce unit cost because setup is spread across more items and production becomes more efficient.

How long does production take?

Typical lead times are 3–12 working days, depending on the item type, quantities and branding method. Premium/import items may require additional time.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and nationwide delivery options. Share your location and deadline for planning.

Do you provide VAT invoices and procurement documentation?

Yes. For tender support, refer to Corporate Profile and Compliance.

How do we start an order?

Start on Contact or WhatsApp. Upload your logo/sample and share quantities and deadline.
