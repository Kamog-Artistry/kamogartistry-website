Main Cost Drivers

1) Item Type & Quality

A pen, mug, bamboo flask, cotton t-shirt, reflector jacket, company overall, tote bag, umbrella, trophy or gift box starts from a different base cost and quality grade.

2) Quantity

Bulk quantities can reduce unit cost where setup, sourcing and branding time are spread across more pieces. Very small quantities may carry higher unit handling costs.

3) Branding Method

Embroidery, DTF, vinyl, screen print, UV print, engraving and sticker branding suit different items, surfaces and budgets.

4) Branding Positions

One logo position is simpler than front and back branding, sleeves, pockets, packaging labels, staff names or department personalisation.

5) Artwork Readiness

Clean vector artwork speeds up quoting and production. Low-resolution logos may need redrawing or layout correction before clean branding.

6) Packaging, Sorting & Delivery

Gift boxes, inserts, wrapping, recipient sorting, branch packing and delivery coordination can change the final quote.

Custom Merchandise Pricing — FAQs

Why are merchandise prices quoted instead of fixed?

Item quality, quantity, supplier availability, branding method, packaging and delivery needs can change the final cost. A tailored quote gives a more accurate total for your exact order.

What details make pricing faster?

Send the item list, quantities, logo files, deadline, delivery location and any reference photos. Budget range also helps us recommend practical options.

Does bulk quantity reduce unit cost?

Often yes. Bulk orders can reduce unit cost because setup, sourcing and production handling are spread across more pieces.

What affects apparel and company overall pricing?

Fabric quality, size mix, logo positions, embroidery, DTF, vinyl, screen print, reflective strip options, names, departments and quantity all affect the quote.

Are bamboo flasks and eco-friendly items priced differently?

Yes. Eco-conscious items such as bamboo flasks, reusable bottles, eco tote bags and recycled-style notebooks are priced by item quality, availability, branding method and quantity.

What affects gift packaging cost?

Gift boxes, bags, inserts, thank-you cards, labels, wrapping, recipient sorting and delivery preparation can change the packaging cost.

Can printed inserts be bundled with merchandise?

Yes. Printed inserts, flyers, cards, brochures and event materials can be coordinated through Printing Services Kampala.

Can you quote from an RFQ or procurement list?

Yes. Upload the RFQ, BOQ, item list or artwork files through the tender/RFQ upload page and we will review the scope before quoting.
