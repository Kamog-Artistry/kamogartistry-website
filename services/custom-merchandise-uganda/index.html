Custom Merchandise Uganda — FAQs

What is custom merchandise?

Custom merchandise includes branded promotional items and gifts such as mugs, t-shirts, caps, notebooks, bottles, umbrellas, lanyards, bags, gift boxes and awards produced for marketing, events, staff kits and corporate gifting.

What are the best promotional items for events in Uganda?

Useful, easy-to-carry items usually work best: notebooks, pens, lanyards, bags, t-shirts, caps, bottles and event packs. The best choice depends on audience, event duration, budget and distribution plan.

How do I choose the right items for my audience?

Start with purpose: awareness, VIP gifting, staff appreciation, partner handover or public campaign. Broad outreach can use pens, notebooks and lanyards. Premium gifting works better with curated gift packages and custom packaging.

What affects the final price of custom merchandise in Uganda?

Pricing depends on item type, quality grade, branding method, logo colours, placement size, quantity, packaging and deadline. Send your item list and quantity through Request Quotation for a tailored quote.

Can you prepare mockups before production?

Yes. We prepare mockups showing logo placement, size and colour before production. You can also upload artwork for review so we check if it is ready for clean branding.

Do you offer corporate gift packaging?

Yes. We create curated gift sets and presentation-ready packaging, including custom gift boxes, printed inserts and branded packaging for VIP gifting or staff kits.

What is the turnaround time for branded merchandise?

Turnaround depends on item availability, quantity, branding method and packaging. For urgent work, WhatsApp your deadline and quantities: message us here.

Can you handle printed inserts, flyers or event materials too?

Yes. If your gift packs need printed inserts, brochures, flyers or event materials, we can coordinate them through Printing Services Kampala.

Do you supply merchandise for NGOs and institutions?

Yes. We produce campaign merchandise, staff kits, training materials, conference packs and outreach items for NGOs, schools, institutions and corporates.

Can you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes. We coordinate delivery across Uganda depending on order size, timeline and destination.

What files should we provide for branding?

Vector files such as AI, EPS, SVG or high-quality PDF are best. PNG files may work for some items, but vector artwork gives cleaner results for most merchandise branding.

How do we start a merchandise order?

Share your items, quantities, branding colours, deadline and delivery location. Start through Request Quotation. Procurement teams can review Corporate Profile and Compliance.
