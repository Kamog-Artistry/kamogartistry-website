How We Handle the Order

1) Clarify the brief

We confirm audience, use case, quantity, deadline, delivery point, artwork and packaging needs.

2) Prepare mockups

We guide logo placement, size, colour and finishing before production starts.

3) Produce and check

We check legibility, placement, alignment and batch consistency across the order.

4) Pack and hand over

We prepare the items for office delivery, event use, staff distribution, field handover or wider dispatch.

NGO Campaign Merchandise Uganda — FAQs

What can be included in NGO campaign merchandise?

Common items include t-shirts, caps, reflector jackets, bags, lanyards, notebooks, pens, bottles, wristbands, flyers, inserts and training materials.

Can you handle donor and partner logo placement?

Yes. We can prepare mockups showing donor, project, partner and implementing organization visibility so branding is approved before production.

Can you prepare field team kits?

Yes. Field team kits can include apparel, caps, reflectors, bags, lanyards, notebooks, bottles and printed materials depending on the project.

Can you support trainings and workshops?

Yes. We can prepare workshop packs, notebooks, pens, lanyards, folders, printed inserts and participant materials.

Can you quote from an RFQ or procurement list?

Yes. Upload the RFQ, BOQ, TOR, item list or artwork files through the tender/RFQ upload page for review before quoting.
