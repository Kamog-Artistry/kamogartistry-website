Prepress Audit Before Your File Goes to Print

Prepress is the quality-control stage between design and production. It reduces the risk of white edges, cut-off text, blurry images, wrong page order, poor black values, weak packaging alignment and supplier rejection. In Kampala print workflows, this matters for brochures, books, catalogues, labels, stickers, packaging, banners, flyers, annual reports, company profiles and event materials.

Bleed & Trim

We check that artwork extends beyond trim and that important text stays safely inside the cut area.

Resolution

We flag low-quality images before they become soft, pixelated or disappointing in print.

Press PDF

We export files in a production-aware way for printer handover, including crop marks where needed.

Packaging Accuracy

Labels and packaging files are checked for size, dieline alignment, bleed and readable small text.

Print-Ready Artwork Kampala — FAQs

What is print-ready artwork?

Print-ready artwork is a file prepared correctly for commercial printing, usually with proper bleed, margins, sizing, image quality and export settings suitable for press.

Can you fix files my printer rejected?

Yes. We can review rejected or problematic files, identify the likely issues and correct them so the artwork is better prepared for production.

Do you only prepare files or can you also design them?

We can do both. If the design is already done, we can prepare it for print. If the piece still needs full design, we can handle the design stage first.

What kinds of print jobs do you prepare artwork for?

Brochures, catalogues, books, flyers, posters, labels, packaging, stickers, company profiles, business proposals, calendars, banners and branded materials.

Can you prepare files for offset and digital printing?

Yes. We can prepare files for offset printing, digital printing, large format output and other production paths depending on the job.

Do you handle packaging and label artwork?

Yes. Print-ready preparation is especially important for packaging and label work where sizing, bleed and technical accuracy affect the final result.

Can you print the finished job after preparing the file?

Yes. If you want one workflow, we can prepare the artwork and connect it to Kamog Artistry printing services.

How do we start a print-ready artwork project?

Share the type of item, existing file, final size if known, printer feedback if available and whether you need file prep only or both prep and printing.
