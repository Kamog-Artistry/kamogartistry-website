Our Catalog Design Process

We keep the process practical: understand the purpose, organise the content, build the page system, refine the layout, then prepare clean final files for sharing or printing. This helps the final catalog feel coherent, persuasive and professionally finished instead of improvised.

1) Brief & Content Review

We review your content, audience, page count, available images, category structure, publication goals and whether the document is intended for print, digital use or both.

2) Structure & Page Planning

We organise the catalog into a logical sequence so the final piece is easier to browse, understand and present.

3) Design & Refinement

We develop the layouts, strengthen visual hierarchy, standardise page treatment, improve typography and refine how the content is experienced.

4) Final Files & Handover

You receive a polished final catalog prepared for the right use case—PDF sharing, internal presentation, commercial printing or all three where needed.

Who This Service Is For (And Who It’s Not For)

Ideal For

  • Retail and wholesale businesses with multiple products to present clearly
  • Manufacturers and distributors introducing product lines and variations
  • Institutions and organisations publishing educational, programme or reference catalogs
  • Corporate businesses presenting service lines or structured product portfolios
  • Event and exhibition teams needing organised leave-behind materials
  • Businesses with outdated catalogs that need redesign and print-aware cleanup

Not For

This service may not fit if you want:

  • A very short promo piece better handled as a flyer or brochure
  • A simple company introduction better suited to a company profile
  • An unstructured item list with no clear categories or usable images
  • A basic document where hierarchy, consistency and production quality do not matter

If you need a more corporate overview instead of a catalog-style document, consider Company Profile Design, Brochure Design or Report Design.

Common Catalog Mistakes We Fix in Kampala

Many catalogs underperform not because the content is weak, but because the document is poorly organised. These are the issues we commonly correct.

Crowded Pages

Too much content forced into weak layouts makes the publication tiring and hard to browse.

Weak Hierarchy

Without clear headings, sections and visual emphasis, readers struggle to follow the flow or compare information.

Inconsistent Presentation

Uneven typography, mixed image treatment and inconsistent page blocks reduce trust and professionalism.

Poor File Preparation

Catalogs prepared without print planning often create margin, colour, output or finishing issues later.

Catalog Design Kampala — FAQs

How much does catalog design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on page count, content volume, layout complexity, image readiness, revision scope and whether the catalog also needs print-ready preparation. For common scopes, see Graphic Design Pricing.

Can you design catalogs for both products and services?

Yes. We design product catalogs, service catalogs, educational publications, institutional catalogs, wholesale catalogs and lookbooks depending on the business model and audience.

Can the final catalog be used both online and in print?

Yes. We can prepare the catalog for digital sharing and, where required, for professional print production as well. If you need printing after design, see Catalog Printing Kampala.

What do I need to provide before catalog design starts?

Usually product or publication content, images, text, categories, specifications, logos, page expectations and any existing reference material or older catalog version.

Can you redesign an old or poorly arranged catalog?

Yes. We can modernise an existing catalog by improving section flow, visual consistency, typography, page hierarchy and overall presentation quality.

What is the difference between a catalog and a brochure?

A brochure is usually shorter and more promotional, while a catalog is often more structured and detailed, especially when presenting a wider range of products, services or editorial content. For shorter promotional pieces, see Brochure Design.

Can you prepare the file for offset or digital printing?

Yes. We can prepare the file correctly for commercial production and also advise on offset printing, digital printing and finishing options depending on quantity and use.

How do we start a catalog design project?

Share your brief, available content, expected size or scope, preferred use case and whether you need digital, print or both. Start here: Contact or WhatsApp us for a quick brief review.
