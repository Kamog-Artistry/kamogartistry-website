Art Exhibition Catalogue Design for Kampala Galleries, Artists and Curators

Art exhibition catalogues need a calmer structure than commercial catalogues. The layout must give artwork space to breathe while still supporting curatorial text, artist statements, titles, dimensions, media, year, pricing notes, acknowledgements and sponsor information. For exhibitions in Kampala and Uganda, the catalogue may also become a record of the show, a sales tool, a press reference and a keepsake for visitors.

Curatorial & Artist Text

Essays, statements, biographies and acknowledgements are given readable hierarchy instead of being squeezed into decorative blocks.

Catalogue Design Kampala — FAQs

Do you design art exhibition catalogues?

Yes. We design art exhibition catalogues for galleries, artists, curators and creative events, including artist bios, artwork captions, curatorial text, sponsor pages and print-ready layouts.

How much does catalogue design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on page count, content volume, layout complexity, image readiness, revision scope and whether the catalogue also needs print-ready preparation.

Can you design catalogues for both products and services?

Yes. We design product catalogues, service catalogues, educational publications, institutional catalogues, wholesale catalogues, lookbooks and exhibition catalogues.

Can the final catalogue be used both online and in print?

Yes. We can prepare a digital PDF for sharing and a separate print-ready file for professional production.

What do I need to provide before catalogue design starts?

Usually images, product or artwork details, text, categories, captions, logos, page expectations and any old catalogue or reference material.

Can you redesign an old or poorly arranged catalogue?

Yes. We can modernise an existing catalogue by improving section flow, visual consistency, typography, page hierarchy and production setup.

What is the difference between a catalogue and a brochure?

A brochure is usually shorter and more promotional, while a catalogue is more structured and detailed, especially when presenting many products, artworks, services or editorial entries.

Can you prepare the file for offset or digital printing?

Yes. We can prepare the file for commercial production and advise on digital printing, offset printing and finishing options depending on quantity and use.
