How Our Design Retainer Process Works

We keep the retainer process practical and structured so it remains useful to the client and manageable for the work itself.

1) Scope & Priorities

We first understand your recurring design needs, brand context, approval structure, preferred turnaround rhythm and the kinds of materials you need most often.

2) Setup & Working Flow

We define how requests will come in, how priorities will be handled, and how work will be organised to support consistency and responsiveness.

3) Ongoing Design Support

We execute the design work across the agreed range of materials while keeping alignment with your brand direction and communication needs.

4) Review & Continuity

Work is refined as needed and used to build a stronger understanding of your brand over time, helping future outputs become more efficient and more consistent.

Design Retainer Kampala — FAQs

What is a design retainer?

A design retainer is an ongoing working arrangement that gives a business recurring access to design support instead of treating every design need as a separate fresh project.

Who is a design retainer best for?

It is best for businesses, organisations and teams that regularly need graphic design support for campaigns, communication materials, print pieces and branded content.

What kinds of work can be covered under a retainer?

Common work includes flyers, posters, brochures, campaign materials, presentations, company document updates, social creatives and print-ready design tasks.

Can a retainer include print-related design support?

Yes. Retainers can include design work that later connects to Printing Services Kampala, depending on the agreed scope.

Is a design retainer better than one-off design requests?

For businesses with recurring needs, it is often more practical because it supports continuity, faster familiarity and more consistent brand output.

Can small businesses use a design retainer?

Yes, especially if they market regularly and need a more dependable design rhythm without building a full internal design team.

How is retainer pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the expected design volume, types of materials involved, workflow complexity and the level of ongoing support required.

How do we start a design retainer?

Share the kinds of materials you need regularly, how often the work comes up, and whether it involves print, digital or both. Start here: Contact or WhatsApp us for a quick discussion.
