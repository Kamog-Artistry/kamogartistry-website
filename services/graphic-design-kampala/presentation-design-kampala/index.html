Slide Storytelling Before Decoration

Many presentations fail because the slides are treated like a document. Long paragraphs, tiny charts and crowded layouts make the presenter work harder and the audience understand less. We design presentations by first improving the story flow, then building the visual system around that structure.

For a pitch deck, this may mean making the problem, solution, market, traction and ask easier to follow. For a corporate presentation, it may mean organising company capability, services, evidence and next steps. For training, it may mean turning dense notes into modules, examples and memorable takeaways.

Audience Path

Slides are arranged around what the audience needs to understand first, second and third.

One Main Point per Slide

We reduce clutter by giving each slide a clear role and takeaway.

Visual Evidence

Charts, numbers, icons, timelines and proof points are designed to support the argument.

Presenter Confidence

The deck should help the speaker present smoothly instead of reading paragraphs from the screen.

Our Presentation Design Process

1) Audience and Goal Review

We review who the deck is for, what decision or understanding it should create, and what content is already available.

2) Story and Slide Planning

We organise the message into a clearer sequence and identify where content should be simplified, grouped or visualised.

3) Slide Design

We design the visual system, improve hierarchy, reduce clutter, refine charts and make the deck feel more polished.

4) Final Export and Handover

You receive the approved deck in the agreed format for presentation, review, sharing or internal reuse.

When Another Format May Work Better

A presentation is best for live or screen-based communication. If the material needs detailed reading, a document may be stronger.

  • Use a proposal for formal submission
  • Use a report for detailed findings
  • Use a company profile for broad introduction
  • Use a catalog for structured product listings

Presentation Problems We Commonly Fix

Crowded Slides

Too much text and too many boxes make the deck hard to present and hard to absorb.

Weak Story Flow

Without logical sequencing, audiences struggle to follow the problem, solution, evidence and action.

Poor Data Design

Charts and numbers lose impact when they are not simplified around a clear takeaway.

Bad Screen Readability

Small fonts and low contrast can fail on projectors, shared screens and meeting-room displays.

Presentation Design Kampala — FAQs

How much does presentation design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on the number of slides, design complexity, content readiness, revision needs and whether the deck needs a reusable branded structure.

Can you design investor decks and corporate presentations?

Yes. We design pitch decks, corporate presentations, investor decks, proposal decks, training slides and structured business presentation systems.

Can the final presentation be shared as PDF too?

Yes. We can prepare the deck for live presentation and also provide a PDF version for sharing, review or follow-up.

What do I need to provide before presentation design starts?

Usually your audience, purpose, existing content, notes, logos, brand references, charts, images and draft slides if available.

Can you redesign an old presentation deck?

Yes. We can modernise an existing presentation by improving slide hierarchy, readability, consistency and story flow.

What is the difference between a presentation and a proposal?

A presentation is usually designed for live delivery or screen-based communication, while a proposal is more document-focused and often meant for formal reading and submission.

Can you align the presentation with our existing brand?

Yes. We can design the deck to align with your current visual identity, templates, logos, colours and brand standards.

How do we start a presentation design project?

Share your audience, presentation goal, available content and whether the deck is for live use, PDF sharing or both.
