Our Presentation Design Process

We keep the process practical: understand the message, organise the content, structure the deck, refine the slides, then prepare clean final files for presentation or sharing. This helps the final presentation feel deliberate and easier to deliver confidently.

1) Brief & Content Review

We review your audience, presentation goal, existing content, brand direction, speaking context, deck length expectations and whether the slides are for live presentation, PDF circulation or both.

2) Story & Slide Planning

We organise the material into a clearer narrative so the presentation makes sense slide by slide and supports stronger audience understanding.

3) Design & Refinement

We build the slides, improve hierarchy, simplify clutter, standardise layouts, refine typography and strengthen the visual impact of the overall deck.

4) Final Files & Handover

You receive a polished presentation file prepared for actual delivery, review or internal reuse depending on your intended format.

Common Presentation Mistakes We Fix in Kampala

Many presentations fail not because the content is weak, but because the slides make it harder to understand the message. These are the issues we commonly correct.

Crowded Slides

Too much text, too many boxes and too little spacing make the deck difficult to present and difficult to absorb.

Weak Story Flow

Without logical sequencing, audiences struggle to understand the problem, solution and value clearly.

Inconsistent Visual Style

Mixed slide types, colours, charts and typography reduce professionalism and distract from the main message.

Poor Screen Readability

Slides designed without presentation conditions in mind can fail on projectors, meeting screens and shared displays.

Presentation Design Kampala — FAQs

How much does presentation design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on the number of slides, design complexity, content readiness, revision needs and whether the deck also needs a reusable branded structure. For common scopes, see Graphic Design Pricing.

Can you design investor decks and corporate presentations?

Yes. We design pitch decks, corporate presentations, investor decks, proposal decks, training slides and structured business presentation systems.

Can the final presentation be shared as PDF too?

Yes. We can prepare the deck for live presentation and also provide a PDF version where needed for sharing, review or follow-up.

What do I need to provide before presentation design starts?

Usually your audience, purpose, existing content, notes, logos, brand references, charts, images and any draft slides if available.

Can you redesign an old presentation deck?

Yes. We can modernise an existing presentation by improving slide hierarchy, readability, consistency and overall story flow.

What is the difference between a presentation and a proposal?

A presentation is usually designed for live delivery or screen-based communication, while a proposal is more document-focused and often meant for formal reading and submission. For document-based submissions, see Business Proposal Design.

Can you align the presentation with our existing brand?

Yes. We can design the deck to align with your current visual identity, templates, logos, colours and brand standards.

How do we start a presentation design project?
