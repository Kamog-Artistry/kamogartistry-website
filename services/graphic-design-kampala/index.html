Our Graphic Design Process

1) Brief & Content

We confirm your goal, audience, content, brand references, size and deadline.

2) Structure & Layout

We design a clean hierarchy—headings, sections, visuals and call-to-action flow.

3) Review & Refinement

You review drafts, we polish typography, spacing, alignment and overall consistency.

4) Final Export (Print + Digital)

We deliver print-ready files (and digital versions) with correct specs for production.

Graphic Design Kampala — FAQs

What graphic design services do you offer in Kampala?

We design company profiles, brochures, catalogs, flyers, posters, menus, reports, proposals, presentations and print-ready artwork. Start with company profiles or brochures.

Can you redesign an existing company profile or brochure?

Yes. Share your current file and content updates. We’ll modernize layout, typography and structure via company profile design or brochure design.

Do you provide print-ready artwork files for printers?

Yes. We prepare correct specs (bleed, margins, resolution) through print-ready artwork. If you also need production, see Printing Services Kampala.

Can you design pitch decks and corporate presentations?

Yes. We design investor decks and corporate slides via presentation design with clean structure and consistent branding.

Do you offer monthly design support for businesses?

Yes. For ongoing needs, choose design retainer support—ideal for busy teams and campaigns.

How long does a brochure or company profile take?

Timing depends on page count, content readiness and revision rounds. WhatsApp your deadline and pages for quick guidance: message us here.

Can you design catalogs for products and services?

Yes. We structure products clearly and ensure consistent layout via catalog design.

How do we start a graphic design project?

Send your brief, content/draft, preferred size (A4/A5/etc), deadline and references. Start via Contact or upload your content.
