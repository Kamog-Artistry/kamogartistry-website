Our Graphic Design Process

1) Brief, Goal & Content Review

We confirm what the design must achieve, who will read it, whether it is for print or digital use, and what content is ready.

2) Structure Before Decoration

We organize headings, sections, page flow, image use and content hierarchy before polishing the look.

3) Design, Review & Refinement

We refine typography, spacing, alignment, colour, visual emphasis and brand consistency based on the approved direction.

4) Final Export, Print Setup or Handoff

We prepare digital versions, print-ready PDFs or production handoff files depending on how the final design will be used.

Graphic Design Pricing in Kampala — What Affects Cost

Design Type & Business Use

A flyer, company profile, proposal, catalogue, report or pitch deck each needs a different level of structure and design attention.

Page Count & Layout Complexity

Longer documents, dense content, tables, charts, captions, product lists and multi-section layouts take more design time.

Content Readiness

Well-prepared text, images and brand assets make the process faster. Rough content may need additional structure before layout begins.

Print-Ready Requirements

Files going to print may require bleed, safe margins, image checks, export setup and finishing-aware preparation.

Deadline & Revision Scope

Rush timelines, many stakeholder reviews or heavy revision rounds can affect scheduling and pricing.
