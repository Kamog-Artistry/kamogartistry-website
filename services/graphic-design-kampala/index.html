Our Graphic Design Process

1) Brief, Objective & Content Review

We confirm the purpose of the design, target audience, preferred format, page count, available content and deadline.

2) Structure & Layout Direction

We organize the content into clear sections, define hierarchy, plan image placement and shape the overall reading flow.

3) Review & Refinement

We refine typography, alignment, spacing, consistency and visual balance based on your feedback.

4) Final Export for Digital or Print

We prepare the final files for the intended use, including print-ready exports where production is required.

Graphic Design Pricing in Kampala — What Affects Cost

Page Count & Layout Complexity

Longer documents, denser content, tables, charts and multi-section layouts take more design time.

Content Readiness

Well-prepared text, images and brand assets make the process faster. We can also help structure rough content.

Deadline & Revision Scope

Rush timelines, many stakeholder reviews or heavy revision rounds can affect scheduling and pricing.
