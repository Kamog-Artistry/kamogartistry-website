Poster Design Built for Viewing Distance and Fast Decisions

Many posters fail because they behave like crowded documents. A viewer may be walking past, standing a few metres away, scrolling quickly on a phone or checking a notice board with many competing messages. The design must give the viewer a clear order: headline first, image or offer second, details third, action last.

We design around the real display context. An event poster needs date, venue and call-to-action clarity. A campaign poster needs message dominance. A retail poster needs the offer to stand out. An academic poster needs credibility and readable details. A movie or entertainment poster needs atmosphere, title impact and visual drama.

Headline First

The main message is given enough weight so viewers understand the poster quickly.

Distance Readability

Font size, contrast and spacing are planned for realistic viewing environments.

Action Clarity

Dates, venues, contacts, QR codes or booking instructions are positioned to be found easily.

Print Confidence

Artwork is prepared with size, bleed and production requirements in mind.

Poster Design Kampala — FAQs

How much does poster design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on poster size, complexity, content readiness, number of variants and whether the artwork also needs print-ready preparation.

Can you design both event and promotional posters?

Yes. We design event posters, promotional posters, campaign posters, advertising posters, corporate notices and awareness posters.

Can the final poster be used both online and in print?

Yes. We can prepare poster artwork for digital sharing and for professional print production where required.

What do I need to provide before poster design starts?

Usually the headline, event or offer details, images, logos, sponsor information, preferred size and display context.

Can you redesign an old poster?

Yes. We can modernise an existing poster by improving hierarchy, visibility, structure, contrast and print readiness.

What is the difference between a poster and a flyer?

A poster is usually designed for display and visibility from a distance, while a flyer is normally for hand distribution and closer reading.

Can you prepare the poster for large-format printing?

Yes. We can prepare artwork for standard poster sizes and larger display formats depending on the intended output.

How do we start a poster design project?

Share your campaign or event details, key message, available images, preferred size and whether you need print, digital or both.
