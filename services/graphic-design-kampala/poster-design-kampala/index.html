Our Poster Design Process

We keep the process practical: understand the purpose, isolate the key message, build strong hierarchy, refine the design, then prepare clean final files for print or digital distribution. This helps the final poster stay focused and easier to notice.

1) Brief & Message Review

We review the poster purpose, audience, main message, event or campaign details, brand direction, format and where the poster will be used.

2) Layout & Hierarchy Planning

We organise the headline, supporting details, imagery and action points so the design communicates quickly and clearly.

3) Design & Refinement

We build the artwork, strengthen contrast, improve typography, refine image placement and make the poster more visible and persuasive.

4) Final Files & Handover

You receive a polished final poster for printing or digital use, prepared in the correct format for its intended display environment.

Common Poster Mistakes We Fix in Kampala

Many posters fail not because the offer is weak, but because the design is not built for fast communication. These are the issues we commonly correct.

Too Much Text

A poster becomes ineffective when readers have to struggle through too much information at once.

Weak Hierarchy

Without a clear headline and supporting order, viewers miss the main message quickly.

Poor Contrast

Weak colour contrast and low-impact typography reduce visibility from a distance.

Poor File Preparation

Artwork prepared without print size and bleed planning often leads to output and quality problems later.

Poster Design Kampala — FAQs

How much does poster design cost in Kampala?

Pricing depends on poster size, complexity, content readiness, number of variants and whether the artwork also needs print-ready preparation. For common scopes, see Graphic Design Pricing.

Can you design both event and promotional posters?

Yes. We design event posters, promotional posters, campaign posters, advertising posters, corporate notice posters and awareness posters depending on the purpose.

Can the final poster be used both online and in print?

Yes. We can prepare the poster for digital sharing and, where required, for professional print production as well. If you need final production, see Poster Printing Kampala.

What do I need to provide before poster design starts?

Usually the headline, event or offer details, images, logos, sponsor information, preferred size and where the poster will be used.

Can you redesign an old poster?

Yes. We can modernise an existing poster by improving hierarchy, visibility, structure, contrast and print readiness.

What is the difference between a poster and a flyer?

A poster is usually designed for display and visibility from a distance, while a flyer is typically meant for hand distribution and closer reading. For handout artwork, see Flyer Design.

Can you prepare the poster for large-format printing?

Yes. We can prepare the artwork correctly for standard poster sizes and larger display formats depending on the intended output.

How do we start a poster design project?

Share your campaign or event details, key message, available images, preferred size and whether you need print, digital or both. Start here: Contact or WhatsApp us for a quick brief review.
