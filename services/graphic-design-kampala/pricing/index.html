What Affects Graphic Design Cost in Kampala

1) Pages / Slides & Structure

More pages, more sections, more layouts—and more consistency checks across the document.

2) Content Readiness

Clean copy saves time. If content is rough, we may first structure and polish it for clarity.

3) Visual Complexity

Infographics, custom icons, diagrams, maps and charts add design time.

4) Image Quality

Low-res images need replacement or editing. Product catalogs can require heavy image work.

5) Revision Rounds

More review cycles and major structural changes increase effort (we keep it transparent).

6) Deadline & Delivery

Rush work may require priority scheduling. Print-ready exports add preflight checks.

Graphic Design Kampala Pricing — FAQs

Do your prices include printing?

This page covers design. If you need printing, we’ll quote production separately (paper, quantity, finishing). See Printing Services Kampala.

What do you need to quote a company profile or proposal accurately?

Page count, content status (ready vs draft), brand assets, and your deadline. Use the Fast Quote Checklist.

Can you redesign an existing PDF or Word document?

Yes. Upload your file and we’ll modernize layout, typography and structure. We can also make it print-ready.

How many revision rounds are included?

We typically include reasonable review rounds based on scope. We agree on review checkpoints upfront—especially for urgent projects.

How fast can you deliver?

Flyers and posters can be same-day depending on complexity. Profiles, catalogs and reports take longer. WhatsApp your deadline for accurate scheduling.

Do you offer monthly design support?

Yes—see Design Retainer Kampala for ongoing monthly output.

Can you match our brand guidelines?

Yes. If you already have a brand, we keep it consistent. If not, start with Branding Kampala and we’ll standardize everything.

How do we start an order?

Start on Contact or WhatsApp. Upload your content/draft and share your page count and deadline.
