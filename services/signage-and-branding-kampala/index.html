Signage & Branding Kampala — FAQs

What information do you need to quote signage in Kampala?

Share the sign type, approximate size, installation location and deadline. Photos of the wall, shopfront or office area make recommendations more accurate.

Do you design and fabricate 3D signage?

Yes. We design and fabricate 3D signs for shopfronts, receptions, offices and branded spaces. See 3D Signage Kampala.

Which is better: LED signage or a lightbox?

LED signage works well for strong visibility and standout presence, while lightbox signage gives a clean illuminated look for retail and roadside identification.

Can you handle shopfront branding and installation?

Yes. We plan shopfront signage, prepare artwork, fabricate the sign and coordinate installation for a clean finish.

Do you produce office signage and wayfinding?

Yes. We produce reception signs, office labels, directional signs and wayfinding systems for offices, clinics, schools, hotels and institutions.

Can you brand walls, windows and floors?

Yes. We produce wall branding, window branding and floor graphics for promotions, privacy, interior identity and guided movement.

How do you keep signage consistent with our brand?

We align colours, typography, layout and materials with your existing identity. If you need identity setup first, visit Branding Kampala.

Do you install signage outside Kampala?

We serve Kampala and can support nearby areas such as Wakiso and Mukono depending on project size, access and installation requirements.

What materials are common for signage?

Common materials include acrylic, aluminium composite panels, vinyl, PVC, LED modules and lightbox materials, depending on whether the sign is indoor or outdoor.

How long does signage production take?

Turnaround depends on size, materials, lighting, approval speed and installation complexity. Share your deadline early so we can recommend the best production path.

Can you use our existing logo artwork?

Yes, but the file must be suitable for production. Vector artwork is preferred. You can upload artwork for review before fabrication.
