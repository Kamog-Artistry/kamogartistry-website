Signage & Branding Kampala — FAQs

What information do you need to quote signage in Kampala?

Share the sign type, approximate size, installation location, and your deadline. Photos of the wall/shop front help a lot. You can start via Contact or WhatsApp, and we’ll advise on materials and placement.

Do you design and fabricate 3D signage?

Yes—see 3D Signage Kampala for built-up letters and premium brand signage. We can recommend finishes based on indoor vs outdoor use.

Which is better: LED signage or a lightbox?

It depends on visibility and your budget. LED signage is great for standout presence, while lightbox signage is clean and readable day/night—especially for retail.

Can you help with shop front branding and installation?

Absolutely. Start with Shop Front Signage and Signage Installation. We handle alignment, mounting, and finishing for a professional look.

Do you do office signage and wayfinding systems?

Yes—ideal for clinics, schools, hotels and offices. Explore Office Signage and Wayfinding Signage to guide visitors clearly.

Can you brand walls, windows, and floors?

Yes. We produce wall branding, window branding, and floor graphics for promotions, privacy, and interior identity.

How do you ensure signage matches our brand identity?

We align colors, fonts, and layout to your brand system. If you need identity setup first, visit Branding Kampala, or review examples in our Portfolio.
