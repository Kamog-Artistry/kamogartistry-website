Why Your Logo Matters (Especially in Kampala’s Competitive Market)

In Kampala, customers compare brands quickly. A weak logo makes a business look unstructured — even if the service is excellent. A good logo builds trust and makes your business feel established.

1) Credibility

A professional logo instantly signals seriousness to customers, suppliers and institutions.

2) Visibility

A logo must stay readable when printed small (receipts) and large (shop signage and banners).

3) Consistency

With a clean logo system, your brand stays consistent across designers, printers and social media managers.

4) Memorability

Customers remember a clear, unique logo faster — which improves referrals and repeat business.

What You Get (Logo Deliverables)

We deliver the logo in formats that work in the real world — not “one PNG” that fails when you go to print or install signage. Your final delivery is organized so any printer or signage team can use it correctly.

  • Print-ready logo files: SVG + PDF (sharp for printing, signage and large format).
  • Digital logo files: PNG (transparent background) + JPG (web use), optimized sizes.
  • Color versions: full color, single color, and black/white versions.
  • Logo variations: primary logo + icon (if applicable) for social/profile usage.
  • Color codes: HEX (digital) + guidance to keep colors consistent in print.
  • Optional add-ons: business card, letterhead, email signature, social templates.

Our Logo Design Process (Simple, Clear, Professional)

We keep the process clear so you get results quickly — without confusion. We also design with implementation in mind, because many logos look “okay” on screen, then fail on signage and print.

1) Brief & Direction

You share your business name, what you do, target audience, and preferred style. If you have references, send them too.

2) Concepts

We propose logo concept directions and explain the thinking behind each.

3) Refinement

We refine your chosen direction: proportions, spacing, balance, readability, and production safety.

4) Final Files + Kit

You receive clean, properly exported files for print and web. If needed, we can apply the logo to stationery and signage.
