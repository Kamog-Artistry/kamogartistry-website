Social Media Branding in Kampala: What We Design (And What Makes It Work)

High-performing social creatives are simple to scan, consistent, and visually disciplined. We build layouts that help your audience understand your offer fast—then we make sure the design still looks clean on WhatsApp compression.

Common social media design needs

  • Promo posts (offers, menus, schedules, service announcements)
  • Campaign creatives (multi-post series with same style)
  • Event posters (clear hierarchy for dates/venues)
  • Brand announcements (new branch, new product, hiring)
  • Special day creatives (New Month, New Week, greetings)
  • Template kits (reusable designs for your team)

What makes social designs convert

  • Hierarchy (headline first, details later)
  • Consistency (same style across weeks)
  • Strong contrast (readability on small screens)
  • Clean spacing (premium look)
  • Correct sizes (story + feed + portrait exports)
  • Brand cues (colors/fonts that become recognisable)

Our Social Media Design Process (Fast + Structured)

Social media work needs speed without losing consistency. We keep the workflow simple so posts come out clean and on time.

1) Brief + content

We collect your text, dates, photos/logos, and the platform targets.

2) Direction

We align designs to your brand style and the goal (promo, event, announcement, campaign).

3) Design + consistency

We build a clean layout system so posts match across the week/month.

4) Revisions

We adjust wording, spacing and hierarchy for clarity and accuracy.

5) Exports

We export in the correct sizes for each platform (story/feed/portrait).

6) Weekly / campaign support

If you want consistency long-term, we run a repeatable schedule for your posts.

Social Media Brand Design Kampala FAQs

Common questions clients ask before ordering social media designs in Kampala.

Can you design posts in all sizes (story + feed)?

Yes. We export in story, square, portrait and landscape where needed—platform-ready.

Do you do weekly or monthly batches?

Yes. Many clients prefer batches for consistency and faster planning.

Can you match our existing brand?

Yes. We follow your colors/fonts—or refine the direction for stronger consistency.

What should I send to start?

Text, dates, photos, logo, brand colors (if any), and the platforms you’ll post on.

Do you provide editable files?

By agreement, yes. Many clients receive exports; editable files can be included where required.

Can you design event posters for social?

Yes. We design event posters with clear hierarchy so people can read fast.

Will designs still look good on WhatsApp?

Yes. We design with readability and compression in mind, then export appropriately.

Can social designs connect to print?

Yes. If you need posters/flyers printed, we can prepare print-ready versions too.

Do you also build a full brand system?

Yes. We can build identity systems and guidelines so everything stays consistent.
