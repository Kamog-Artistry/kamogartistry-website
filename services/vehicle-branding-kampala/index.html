Vehicle Branding Kampala — FAQs

What’s the difference between decals and a full vehicle wrap?

Decals are cut or printed graphics applied to selected areas (logos, contacts, messages). A wrap covers larger surfaces (partial or full) for higher impact. See Vehicle Decals and Vehicle Wraps.

How long does vehicle branding last in Kampala?

Lifespan depends on material, lamination, exposure and washing habits. We recommend durable finishes for daily-use vehicles and can advise the best option after reviewing your photos and use-case.

Do you help with design, or can I bring my own artwork?

Both. We can design from scratch with mockups via Vehicle Branding Design, or review and adapt your files to be production-ready. Upload files on Contact.

Can you brand multiple vehicles (fleet branding)?

Yes. We create a consistent system across all vehicles and variants (cars, vans, trucks). Start with Fleet Vehicle Branding.

Do you offer reflective graphics for night visibility?

Yes—reflective vinyl improves visibility and can also boost brand presence at night. See Reflective Vehicle Branding.

How do I prepare my vehicle before installation?

A clean surface helps adhesion and finish. We’ll guide you on preparation and timing during booking. For a clean outcome, see Installation.

Can you combine vehicle branding with printing or signage?

Yes. Many clients pair fleet branding with business cards, brochures, banners or storefront signage. Explore Printing Services and Signage Fabrication.

How do we start vehicle branding with Kamog Artistry?

Send vehicle photos (front/side/back), your logo (if available), and what you want to communicate (contacts, slogan, services). Start here: Contact. Procurement teams can review Corporate Profile and Compliance.
