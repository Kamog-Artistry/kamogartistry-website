Vehicle Branding Kampala — FAQs

What is vehicle branding?

Vehicle branding is the application of business graphics on cars, vans, trucks, buses or bikes. It can include logos, contacts, services, slogans, wraps, decals, reflective vinyl and window graphics.

What is the difference between decals and a full vehicle wrap?

Decals are graphics applied to selected areas such as doors, windows or panels. A full wrap covers larger surfaces for stronger visual impact and a more complete branded look.

How much does vehicle branding cost in Kampala?

Cost depends on vehicle size, coverage, materials, reflective options, design work and installation. Send vehicle photos for a more accurate quote.

How long does vehicle branding last?

Lifespan depends on material quality, lamination, sunlight exposure, washing habits and how often the vehicle is used. We recommend suitable finishes for daily-use vehicles.

Do you design the vehicle branding layout?

Yes. We can design from scratch using your logo and message, or refine existing artwork into production-ready vehicle graphics.

Can you brand multiple vehicles?

Yes. Fleet branding is ideal for companies with several vehicles. We create a repeatable system so each vehicle looks consistent even when vehicle sizes differ.

Do you offer reflective vehicle branding?

Yes. Reflective vinyl improves night visibility and is useful for logistics, schools, emergency support, field teams and commercial fleets.

Can you brand windows without blocking visibility?

Yes. Window graphics can use suitable materials such as perforated vinyl depending on the vehicle and visibility requirements.

How should I prepare my vehicle before installation?

The vehicle should be clean and ready for application. We share preparation guidance when booking the installation so the vinyl adheres properly.

Can you combine vehicle branding with printing and signage?

Yes. Many clients combine vehicle branding with business cards, brochures, banners, stickers and signage so campaigns remain consistent.

Do you serve clients outside Kampala?

Yes. We serve Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and wider Uganda depending on project size, schedule and installation requirements.
