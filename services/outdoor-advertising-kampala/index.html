Outdoor Advertising Kampala — FAQs

What outdoor advertising services do you offer in Kampala?

We produce billboards, banners, street pole branding, building wraps, roadside boards, directional signage and large format prints for campaigns and business visibility.

Can you design outdoor ads that are readable from a distance?

Yes. Outdoor layouts need short copy, strong contrast and clear hierarchy. We prepare artwork that can be understood quickly by people walking, driving or entering an event space.

Do you handle installation for outdoor signage?

Where required, we coordinate installation and provide print-ready assets. Share your location, sizes and deadline through Contact or WhatsApp.

Which material is best for outdoor banners in Kampala weather?

The best material depends on placement, campaign duration and exposure. We recommend suitable outdoor-grade materials and finishing after reviewing your location and use-case.

Can you provide wayfinding signs for events and venues?

Yes. We design and produce directional signage and wayfinding systems for events, venues, institutions and public-facing spaces.

What affects outdoor advertising pricing?

Pricing depends on size, material, print coverage, finishing, quantity and whether installation coordination is required.

Can outdoor ads be bundled with printing and event branding?

Yes. Many campaigns combine outdoor displays with printing, event branding, signage and branded handouts.

How do we start an outdoor advertising order?

Send the size, location, quantity, deadline and message. You can also upload artwork for review before production.

Can you adapt existing artwork for billboard or banner size?

Yes. We can resize and prepare existing artwork for large format output, then check resolution, safe areas and readability.

Do you work on real estate and construction site boards?

Yes. We produce property boards, site boards, safety signs and project branding for real estate, construction and infrastructure projects.
