1) Plan & Sizes

Event Branding Kampala — FAQs

What items are included in event branding?

Common items include stage backdrops, step & repeat walls, roll-up banners, booth graphics, directional signage, badges/lanyards, programmes and promotional posters. Start with event backdrops and we’ll recommend the full set.

How early should we start event branding in Kampala?

The earlier the better—especially for large items and multi-piece booth setups. For urgent events, we can prioritise production-ready layouts and fast print options. Share your date via Contact or WhatsApp for a quick plan.

Do you design and print backdrops and banners?

Yes. We design with correct sizes, bleed and readability, and can coordinate printing/finishing for clean results. See event banners and step & repeat backdrops.

Can you brand an exhibition booth or stand?

Yes—booth wall panels, pop-ups, counters and stand graphics. We help structure messaging so it reads fast and looks premium on camera. Explore booth branding.

Do you provide directional signage for event flow?

Yes—registration, halls, restrooms, parking, VIP routes and more. Clear wayfinding improves guest experience and reduces confusion. See event directional signage.

Can you handle badges, programmes and posters?

Yes. We design for readability, role hierarchy and print durability. See badges & lanyards and programmes.

How much does event branding cost in Kampala?

Cost depends on the number of items, sizes, materials/finishing, quantities and timeline. For print-heavy scopes, you can also review Printing Services Kampala. Request a quote via Contact.

How do we start with Kamog Artistry?

Send your event name, date, venue, sponsor list (if any), and the items you need. We’ll recommend sizes, materials and the most effective set. Start here: Contact. Corporate teams can review Corporate Profile and Compliance.
