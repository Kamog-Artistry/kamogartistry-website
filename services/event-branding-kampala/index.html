Event Branding Kampala — FAQs

What items are included in event branding?

Common items include stage backdrops, step and repeat walls, roll-up banners, booth graphics, directional signage, badges, lanyards, programmes and promotional posters.

How early should we start event branding in Kampala?

Start as early as possible, especially for large backdrops, booth setups and printed programme sets. For urgent events, send the event date and required items on WhatsApp.

Do you design and print backdrops and banners?

Yes. We design with correct sizes, bleed, safe margins and readability, then prepare clean production-ready artwork for printing and finishing.

Can you brand an exhibition booth or stand?

Yes. We create booth wall panels, pop-ups, counters and stand graphics with messaging structured for fast reading and premium presentation.

Do you provide directional signage for event flow?

Yes. We prepare wayfinding signage for registration, halls, restrooms, parking, VIP routes and guest flow.

Can you handle badges, programmes and posters?

Yes. We design and prepare print-ready badges, programmes and posters with clear hierarchy and production-safe layout settings.

How much does event branding cost in Kampala?

Cost depends on item count, sizes, materials, finishing, quantities and timeline. View Event Branding Pricing for common ranges.

How do we start with Kamog Artistry?

Send your event date, venue, sponsor list and required items through Contact or WhatsApp. We’ll recommend sizes, materials and a production plan.
