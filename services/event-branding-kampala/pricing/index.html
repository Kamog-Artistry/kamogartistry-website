Fast Quote Checklist

Send these details for a faster and more accurate event branding quote.

  • Event name, date and venue: include setup date if different.
  • Items needed: backdrop, sponsor wall, banners, booth, signage, badges, programmes or posters.
  • Sizes: for example 2×3m backdrop, 3×4m stage wall or 85×200cm roll-up.
  • Quantity: number of each item.
  • Material: PVC banner, fabric, foamboard, sticker or corflute, plus indoor/outdoor use.
  • Finishing: eyelets, poles, stand hardware, frame, lamination or clips.
  • Design status: ready artwork, needs design or sponsor logos available.
  • Delivery or installation: pickup, delivery location or on-site setup support.
  • Deadline: when everything must be ready.

What Affects Event Branding Cost in Kampala

Sizes & Quantity

Large stage walls and multiple items increase material use and finishing time. Quantity can reduce unit cost.

Material Choice

PVC banner, fabric, foamboard, corflute and sticker materials all have different durability and cost.

Hardware & Frames

Roll-up bases, stands and frames affect pricing, especially for reusable event assets.

Finishing

Eyelets, pole pockets, lamination, double-sided prints, sleeves and mounts add production steps.

Artwork Readiness

Ready artwork is faster. Sponsor-heavy layouts may need logo cleanup and layout preparation.

Timeline & Delivery

Rush timelines and delivery requirements can affect production cost and planning.

Event Branding Kampala Pricing — FAQs

What do you need to quote an event backdrop or sponsor wall?

Share size, material, whether it is print-only or with frame, finishing requirements and deadline. Use the Fast Quote Checklist.

Do these prices include design?

The ranges focus mainly on production. Design is quoted based on complexity, sponsor logos, number of layouts and approval rounds.

How fast can you deliver event branding in Kampala?

Some items can be fast if artwork is ready. Larger scopes, frames, booth sets and programmes usually need more time.

Can you handle booth branding for exhibitions?

Yes. Booth wall graphics, counter branding, stand panels, roll-ups and flyers can be planned as one consistent set.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes. We serve Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and wider Uganda depending on project type, timeline and delivery needs.

How do you keep sponsor logos clean?

We request vector or high-resolution logos, standardise spacing and sizing, then provide a proof for approval before production.

Do you provide procurement documents or VAT invoices?

Yes. Corporate and institutional teams can review Corporate Profile and Compliance.

How do we start?

Start through Contact or WhatsApp. Share event date, venue, sizes, quantities and artwork status.
