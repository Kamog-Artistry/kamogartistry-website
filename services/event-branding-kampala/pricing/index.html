What Affects Event Branding Cost in Kampala

1) Sizes & Quantity

Large stage walls and multiple items increase material and finishing time. Quantity often reduces unit cost.

2) Material Choice

PVC banner vs fabric vs foamboard vs corflute—each has different cost and durability.

3) Hardware & Frames

Roll-up bases, frames and stands significantly affect pricing (especially for reusable assets).

4) Finishing

Eyelets, pole pockets, lamination, double-sided prints, pockets, sleeves and specialty mounts add steps.

5) Artwork Readiness

Ready, print-safe artwork is faster. Sponsor-heavy designs may need cleanup and layout rules.

6) Timeline & Delivery

Rush approvals and deadlines can increase production cost. Delivery depends on location and volume.

Event Branding Kampala Pricing — FAQs

What do you need to quote an event backdrop or step & repeat?

Size (e.g., 2×3m / 3×4m), whether it is print-only or with frame/stand, material (PVC/fabric), finishing (eyelets/pockets), and your deadline. Use the Fast Quote Checklist.

Do you include design in these prices?

Pricing ranges focus on production. If you have ready artwork, we can print fast. If you need design (especially sponsor-heavy layouts), we quote design based on complexity and number of items.

How fast can you deliver event branding in Kampala?

Some items (roll-ups, boards) can be fast if artwork is ready. Larger scopes (frames, booth sets, programmes) may take longer. If urgent, WhatsApp your sizes, quantities and date.

Can you handle booth branding for exhibitions?

Yes. We structure messaging to read quickly and look premium. Explore Booth Branding and Exhibition Stand Design.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes—Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono and central Uganda coverage. Share delivery location and deadline for planning.

How do you ensure sponsor logos look clean on the wall?

We request clean logos (vector or high-res), standardize sizing and spacing, and keep safe margins for photos. For complex sponsor lists, we can propose a layout system and approval proof.

Do you provide procurement documentation / VAT invoices?

Yes. For tender support, refer to Corporate Profile and Compliance.

How do we start?

Start on Contact or WhatsApp. Share your event date, venue, sizes and items needed. Upload artwork or sponsor logos for review.
