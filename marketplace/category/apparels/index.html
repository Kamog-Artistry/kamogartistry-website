Apparels FAQs

Quick answers to common questions before you add items to cart or request a quotation.

Can I mix sizes on staff or event apparel orders?

Yes. For many caps, T-shirts, polo shirts, jackets and overalls, sizes can usually be mixed within the same order depending on product type and branding method.

Do you handle small runs as well as bulk apparel orders?

Yes. You can order a few pieces for samples or smaller events, and you can also request larger branded quantities for campaigns, schools, company teams and field activations.

Which apparel items work best for uniforms?

Embroidered polo shirts, premium polos, reflective jackets and overalls are some of the best options for staff uniforms, technical teams, hospitality teams and branded company wear.

Can I add other branded products with my apparel order?

Yes. You can pair apparel with mugs, umbrellas, pens, business cards, banners and signage so your order supports uniforms, events, giveaways and branded visibility together.
