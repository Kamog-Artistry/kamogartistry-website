Branded merchandise FAQs

Quick answers for common merchandise questions before you proceed to cart or quote request.

Can I order merchandise in smaller quantities?

Yes. Many mugs, flasks and umbrellas can be handled in smaller quantities depending on stock, artwork and branding method.

Are these good for office gifts and giveaways?

Yes. This category works well for appreciation packs, conference handouts, campaign souvenirs, school branding and desk-friendly office items.

Can I combine merchandise with other branded items?

Yes. You can add merchandise to cart and continue browsing apparels, stationery, banners and signage before checkout or quote request.

What is a strong premium merchandise option?

Travel mugs, insulated bamboo flasks and premium paired stationery items are strong choices for executive gifts and high-value presentation packs.
