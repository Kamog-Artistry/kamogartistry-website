Delivery and installation

Order support varies by product type, quantity and whether on-site setup is needed.

Apparels

Delivery is available in Kampala and across Uganda. Bulk apparel orders are usually packed by size and product type for easier sorting on arrival.

Branded Merchandise

Fragile items are wrapped and boxed for dispatch. Local delivery and upcountry courier options are available where needed.

Banners & Event Displays

Display hardware can be delivered assembled or packed. Basic setup is straightforward, while larger backdrop systems may need on-site support.

Branded Stationery

Stationery orders can be bundled by item type and quantity for office delivery or dispatch. Installation is usually not required.

Signage

Bulky signage may require dedicated transport. Professional installation is recommended for mounted, fabricated or illuminated signs.

Payments and order approval

Standard products can move through the normal checkout flow, while more complex signage and custom jobs are better handled through the quotation process first.

FAQs

Quick answers to common marketplace questions, with helpful next steps from around the Kamog Artistry website.

Can I mix sizes on apparel orders?

Yes. For many polos, T-shirts, jackets and uniforms, we can usually combine sizes within the same order. For the best fit across your team, browse our Apparels category or visit our Custom Merchandise service page for a wider production view.

Do you handle small runs?

Yes. A lot of items, especially mugs, caps, business cards and selected stationery, can be produced in smaller quantities. If you want to compare options first, visit our Custom Merchandise page, our Branded Stationery service, or see recent work on the portfolio.

Are banner systems easy to set up?

Yes. Roll-up banners, X-stands and many portable display systems are designed to be practical and quick to use. If you need a fuller event setup, visit our Event Branding page or our Large Format Printing page for related display guidance.

Can receipt books be numbered?

Yes. Receipt books and invoice books can usually be customised with numbering, branding and quantity options based on your job specifications. Visit our Branded Stationery page or our Offset Printing page if you want a more detailed reference before ordering.

Does signage pricing include installation?

Not always. Final signage pricing depends on size, site conditions, transport, access and mounting requirements. For a clearer picture, visit our Signage & Branding service, read our signage cost guide, or explore completed work on the portfolio.

Can I order without finished artwork?

Yes. If your artwork is not ready yet, we can still guide the job through design, print preparation and production. Visit our Graphic Design page, our Branding service, or go straight to the quote request page and tell us what you need.
