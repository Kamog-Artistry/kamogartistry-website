FAQs

Helpful answers before you move into a category or combine products across the marketplace.

Can I combine products from different categories in one order?

Yes. You can browse multiple categories, add products to cart and build one order across apparel, merchandise, stationery, displays and signage.

What if I am not sure which category fits my job?

Start with the closest category, then use the quote request route if your order spans more than one product type or needs guidance.

Are all categories suitable for small and bulk orders?

Many products support both small and larger orders, but the most suitable route depends on the item, branding method and production scope.

Can I request design help before ordering?

Yes. If artwork is not ready, you can still browse categories, shortlist products and request a quote for design and production support.

Ready to move from browsing to ordering?

Open a category, add the products you need, then review your cart or send a quote request for mixed orders, custom branding or large production work.
