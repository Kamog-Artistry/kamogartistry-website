Pricing & Turnaround (What Affects Cost + Timelines)

Most delays and reprints happen when sizes, materials, finishes and approvals are unclear. Here’s how we keep the process predictable.

What affects pricing

• Size & quantity
• Materials (paper / vinyl / acrylic / metal)
• Finishing (lamination, UV, emboss, binding)
• Installation complexity (for signage)
• Urgency / timelines
• Design needs (new vs supplied artwork)

Share sizes + quantity + finish preference (if any), and we’ll provide clear options.

Turnaround expectations

Timelines depend on scope and approvals. We always confirm schedule before production starts.

• Design proofs: after brief confirmation
• Print: after approval + production slot
• Signage: depends on fabrication + installation
• Events: schedule around your event date

For urgent jobs, contact us early so we advise the fastest reliable option.
