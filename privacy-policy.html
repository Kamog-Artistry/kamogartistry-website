Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kamog Artistry (U) Ltd handles the details you share when requesting branding, printing, signage, design, procurement or production support.

We use that information to respond, quote, prepare artwork, confirm specifications and deliver the work you requested. We do not sell client or visitor information.

1. What We May Receive From You

Most of the information we receive is shared by you directly. This may happen when you call, send a WhatsApp message, email us, request a quotation, fill a contact form, upload artwork or ask for company documents for procurement review.

  • Your name, phone number, email address, company or organization name.
  • Project details such as size, quantity, material, location, deadline, preferred finish and budget guidance.
  • Logos, brand files, artwork, photographs, references, print-ready files, tender notes or documents needed to review the job.
  • Approvals, revisions, delivery instructions, payment references and normal business communication linked to your project.
  • Basic website information such as pages visited, browser type, device details and server logs used to keep the site working well.

2. How We Use That Information

We use the information to serve you properly. For example, we may need your logo to check print quality, your quantity to price the job, your location to plan delivery, or your deadline to confirm whether production is realistic.

  • To reply to inquiries and prepare quotations, proposals, invoices or delivery notes.
  • To check artwork, confirm dimensions, advise on materials and avoid production mistakes.
  • To coordinate design, printing, signage fabrication, branding, delivery and installation.
  • To follow up on approvals, revisions, payments, collection, delivery and after-service support.
  • To keep practical business records for accounting, procurement, tax, warranty, repeat orders and dispute resolution.

3. Artwork, Logos & Project Files

Because our work involves branding, printing, signage and creative production, clients often send us original logos, editable files, photos, text content, brand guidelines or design references. We treat these as project materials, not as content to expose unnecessarily.

We use project files to quote, design, proof, print, fabricate, install, deliver or revise the work you requested. Confidential files, tender documents, bank details, identification documents and private procurement records are not intentionally published on our website or social media.

For completed work, we may document projects for our portfolio or case studies where it is appropriate. If a project is confidential, under an NDA, not yet public, or should not appear in our portfolio, please tell us clearly before or during the project.

For repeat jobs, we may keep useful production details such as the approved size, material, colour reference or finishing instruction so that future orders can be faster and more consistent. This is especially helpful for stationery, signage, uniforms, menus, labels and branded merchandise where accuracy matters.

4. Who May Access Project Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information only when it is needed to deliver the work or meet a lawful/business requirement.

For example, a production partner may need approved artwork, a delivery rider may need a contact number and delivery location, or an email/form provider may process the message you send through the website. In each case, we aim to share only what is needed for that task.

Internal access is also limited by purpose. A designer may need artwork, a production person may need size and material instructions, while an accounts contact may need invoice and payment details. Not every person involved in a project needs every detail.

5. Website Data, Cookies & External Links

Our website may use basic cookies, analytics, form tools and server logs to understand how visitors use the site, improve navigation, secure the website and know which pages are useful to clients.

Some links may take you to WhatsApp, email, Google services, social media pages or other external platforms. Those platforms handle data under their own privacy policies, so we encourage you to review their terms when using them.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably useful for the project, repeat orders, accounting, tax, procurement records, customer support or legal/business needs. Some files may be archived for reference, while files that are no longer needed may be deleted during normal housekeeping.

We take reasonable care to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. Still, no website, email inbox, WhatsApp chat or online file transfer is completely risk-free. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information unless it is required for a quotation, production process or official onboarding.

7. Your Choices

You can ask us to correct inaccurate details, confirm whether we still hold information linked to your project, delete information where practical, or avoid publishing your project in our portfolio when confidentiality applies.

Some records may still be kept where needed for accounting, tax, legal, procurement, payment verification, delivery proof or legitimate business reasons.

Privacy FAQs

Do you sell client information?

No. We do not sell client or visitor information. We use it to respond, quote, produce, deliver, support and keep necessary business records.

Can I ask you not to publish my project?

Yes. Tell us when the work is confidential or when portfolio use is not allowed. We respect confidentiality where it is agreed or reasonably required.

Do you keep uploaded artwork?

We may keep artwork for active production, revisions, repeat orders and record keeping. Files that are no longer needed may be archived or deleted.

How do I request correction or deletion?

Email info@kamogartistry.com or use the Contact page. Some records may be retained where legally or commercially necessary.

Need to contact us about privacy or a project file?

Share the page, file name, project reference or message thread involved so we can review your request quickly.
