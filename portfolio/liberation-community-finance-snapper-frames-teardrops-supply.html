Project Summary

Display-Ready Supply for a Public-Facing Financial Institution

Liberation Community Finance Ltd needed branch-ready branding materials that could help customers read loan information, understand services, scan the app QR code and recognise the brand from the office compound. The assignment was therefore not loose poster printing. It was a finished display supply: snapper frames with vinyl artwork already mounted and fitted, plus 6 pieces of 4.5m telescopic/L-shape teardrop banners prepared with hardware for branch presence.

In financial institutions, office display materials must do more than decorate a wall. They help create a safer, more orderly and better-informed customer space where loan products, app access, financial-literacy messages and institutional values can be seen without staff repeating every detail from scratch.

The Brief

Make the Branch Space Clear, Useful and Visible

The client needed display materials for a real office environment: readable at customer distance, clean enough for a financial institution, easy to maintain and strong enough to support both indoor communication and outdoor visibility. The content covered responsible borrowing, loan products, core values, vision and mission, available services and the Liberation App.

Customer Guidance

Borrowing guidance and financial literacy messages were presented in a visible format for the branch environment.

Loan Product Awareness

School fees, group, micro business and other loan-service visuals were prepared for quick customer recognition.

App Access

The Liberation App artwork included a QR code prepared and checked for practical scanning.

Branch Presence

Six 4.5m telescopic/L-shape teardrop banners supported road-facing and compound visibility around the Namirembe office.

Liberation Community Finance Ltd provides community finance and microfinance services through branch-level support and digital channels. Customers looking for school fees loans, group loans, micro business loans, salary loans, app access or branch information can visit Liberation Community Finance. Kamog Artistry’s role in this project was to prepare the physical visibility materials that help such services communicate clearly inside and around the branch.

Production Process

From Artwork Setup to Finished Display Materials

This was a display-ready institutional branding assignment. Every item had to move beyond artwork and printing into usable physical materials: clearly readable, neatly fitted, protected, packed and ready for the client’s office environment.

FAQs

Snapper Frames, Replaceable Artwork, QR Displays and Teardrop Banner Questions

Practical answers for institutions planning office wall displays, wall-of-fame branding, loan product notices, customer guidance boards, QR-enabled app promotion and outdoor branch visibility.

Can Kamog Artistry supply snapper frames in Kampala and Uganda?

Yes. Kamog Artistry (U) Ltd supplies A1, A2 and other snapper frame sizes for offices, receptions, branches, schools, clinics, NGOs, financial institutions and corporate spaces that need clean, professional wall display systems.

What materials can be used for snapper frame artwork?

Besides vinyl print artwork, we can also prepare artwork using pull-up banner material and other suitable display materials depending on the space, expected durability, finish required and how often the client plans to replace the artwork.

Do you print and fit artwork into snapper frames?

Yes. We can handle artwork setup, material selection, printing, frame opening, artwork mounting or fitting, protective sheet handling, cleaning, packing and delivery readiness so the frames arrive ready for use.

Are snapper frames good for wall-of-fame branding?

Yes. Snapper frames work well for wall-of-fame branding, staff recognition, branch notices, product displays, service boards and policy communication because artwork can be swapped from the front without removing the frame from the wall.

Why are snapper frames useful for frequently changing office communication?

They allow quick artwork replacement. Instead of removing the frame, you simply open the snapper edges, replace the artwork and close it again, making them practical for campaigns, loan offers, compliance notices, school notices and customer information boards.

Can snapper frames be used by financial institutions and SACCOs?

Yes. They are strong for loan products, financial literacy messages, app promotion, branch services, values, mission statements, compliance notices and customer guidance in banks, SACCOs, MFIs and community finance offices.

Do you supply teardrop, feather and tear-shape banners?

Yes. Besides the 6 pieces of 4.5m telescopic/L-shape teardrop banners delivered to Liberation Community Finance Ltd, Kamog Artistry can prepare feather-shaped banners, tear-shape teardrop banners and telescopic/L-shape teardrop banners for branch visibility, activations, road-facing branding and outdoor campaigns.

What sizes are available for teardrop and feather banners?

Feather-shaped banners, tear-shape teardrop banners and telescopic/L-shape teardrop banners are available in Small, Medium and Large size categories depending on viewing distance, placement area, wind exposure, budget and brand visibility target.

Can you test QR codes before print production?

Yes. QR codes can be integrated into app, website, payment or campaign artwork and tested before production. This project included QR scan testing for the Liberation App display to keep the code clear and usable even with the protective sheet on the frame.

Can you handle urgent institutional branding supply?

Yes. This case shows a coordinated workflow covering artwork setup, vinyl printing, snapper frame fitting, teardrop preparation, packing and delivery readiness under a tight deadline for an institutional client.
