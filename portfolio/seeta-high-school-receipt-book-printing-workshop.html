Challenge

A Workshop Project That Needed Both Promotion and Administrative Control

The school required materials that could support two different tasks at once. First, the workshop needed a poster capable of communicating its theme, date, venue, fee and contact information clearly. Second, the institution needed receipt books that could be used confidently during registration and payment handling without looking generic or poorly structured.

Clear workshop communication

The poster needed to organise the workshop theme, participation fee, location details, dates and contacts into a layout that could be understood quickly by teachers and other viewers.

Functional receipt layout

The receipt books needed fields for payer details, amount paid, payment purpose, figures, balance and signature while remaining neat, formal and easy to use manually.

Institution-ready output

The final materials had to feel official and dependable in real use, not merely attractive as digital concepts.

This case study demonstrates how branded communication design and practical institutional printing can work together: one set of materials supports visibility and attendance, while another supports payment documentation, control and proper record keeping during the same programme.
Approach

Design, Layout Logic and Print Preparation Built as One Workflow

The approach combined visual hierarchy, campus-specific adaptation and production thinking. Poster design focused on readability and event emphasis. Receipt book design focused on layout clarity, institutional branding and functional field structure. Artwork was then prepared for reliable production as printed workshop materials.

Poster concept and information hierarchy

The workshop poster was structured to place the title, fee, venue and contact information in a clear sequence for faster understanding.

Receipt layout design

Main Campus and Mukono Campus layouts were organised with clear fields for names, sums, balances, signatures and workshop payment descriptions.

Print-ready artwork preparation

Artwork guides and layout refinements were used to prepare the materials for dependable physical output and consistent finishing.

Printed production and delivery

The finished receipt books were produced and delivered as usable institutional materials for live workshop administration.

Projects like this naturally connect with event branding, institutional stationery printing, graphic design services and print-ready artwork preparation.

Outcome

Workshop Materials That Supported Both Visibility and Working Administration

The finished outputs delivered a stronger public-facing workshop communication piece alongside structured printed materials designed for real fee collection and workshop administration. That combination made the project valuable not only as branded design work, but also as a practical institutional toolset.

Better workshop visibility

The poster brought the event message together in a cleaner and more confident school-branded presentation.

Clearer payment documentation

The receipt books provided a structured printed format for workshop payment collection, participant handling and record support.

Dependable print execution

The project shows Kamog Artistry’s ability to move from layout development into finished physical production for institutional clients.
