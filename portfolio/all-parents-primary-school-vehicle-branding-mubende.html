Challenge

Turning a Practical School Van into a Clear Mobile Identity System

School vehicles need to be easy to recognise, especially in daily transport use. For this project, the available logo needed to be cleaned up and reconstructed so it could scale properly for the van. The lettering had to remain readable, the education-themed icons needed to fit the vehicle surface, and the final graphics had to be applied cleanly despite real field conditions.

Low-resolution logo source

The original crest needed vector preparation so it could reproduce clearly on the vehicle without losing shape, color clarity or edge quality.

Readable school transport identity

The design needed to make the school name, Mubende identity and contact details readable from normal viewing distance while keeping the van visually balanced.

Night installation and weather interruption

During the night installation phase, rain interrupted part of the work. Because vinyl graphics require dry surfaces for strong adhesion and clean finishing, the team paused and completed the remaining work the following day.

This case study documents the practical side of vehicle branding: logo preparation, artwork planning, material production, vinyl application and field judgement. It shows why strong vehicle graphics depend on both design decisions and installation discipline.
Approach

From Raster Logo to Vector Artwork, Printed Vinyl and On-Site Vehicle Graphics Installation

The workflow started with logo redrawing and artwork preparation before moving into production and installation. Each step was handled as part of one practical vehicle branding system so the final output could look organized, readable and durable enough for daily school transport use.

Logo redrawing and vector preparation

The school logo was rebuilt from a raster source into cleaner vector artwork so it could scale correctly for the van graphics and print sharply.

Vehicle graphics layout and mockup

The school name, crest, contact information and education-themed symbols were arranged for side and rear visibility before production.

Print, lamination, plotting and weeding

The artwork was prepared for output, printed, laminated with a glossy protective finish before plotting, then weeded so the graphics could be applied cleanly on the vehicle surface.

Field installation and completion

The team installed the graphics on-site, paused when rain affected the night work conditions, and returned the following day to complete the final branding.

Why School Vehicle Branding Matters for Institutions, Transport Teams and Public Recognition

A school van is more than transport. It is a moving public identity. When the vehicle carries a clean logo, readable school name and properly placed contact details, it helps parents, pupils and the community recognize the institution faster. For schools and organizations, this kind of branding supports visibility, trust and everyday professionalism.

More professional public presentation

Industry Context

Vehicle Branding Works Best When Design, Production and Installation Are Treated as One System

Strong vehicle branding has to survive real movement, surfaces, weather and viewing distance. It is not enough for graphics to look good on a computer screen. They must be readable from the road, sized correctly for the vehicle, prepared properly for production and installed under suitable surface conditions.

In Uganda, branded vehicles support schools, NGOs, delivery companies, corporate fleets, service businesses and field teams. The best outcomes usually come when the branding partner understands design, printing, vinyl preparation and installation together.

Design for real viewing distance

Lettering, hierarchy and logo size must work while the vehicle is parked, moving slowly or seen briefly on the road.

Production files matter

Clean artwork, vector graphics and correct output preparation reduce production mistakes and improve the final look.

Installation conditions matter

Dry, clean surfaces help vinyl graphics adhere properly and preserve a cleaner finish over time.
