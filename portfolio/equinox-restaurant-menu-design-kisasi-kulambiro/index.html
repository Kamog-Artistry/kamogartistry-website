Restaurant Menu Design • Kisasi Kulambiro

Equinox Restaurant Menu Design Printed menu layout, food category organisation and QR menu access for the restaurant section at Equinox Sport & Fitness Center.

Kamog Artistry designed a restaurant menu presentation for Equinox in Kisasi Kulambiro, turning a long food list into a more organised printed menu system with cover artwork, inside spreads, food imagery, readable prices and a back-cover QR entry point for guests who prefer to view the menu on phone.

The project was built around a simple customer experience: open the printed menu at the table, scan from the back cover when needed, or download the full PDF version for easy sharing.

Design Brief

The menu had to feel appetising, but still work like a practical ordering tool

A restaurant menu is not only decoration. It has to guide a customer through the food offer, make prices easy to compare, and make each section feel intentional. For Equinox, the page structure needed to hold breakfast, sandwiches, salads, soups, chicken, beef, goat, pizza, burgers, fish, appetizers and accompaniments without losing readability.

What the design needed to solve

  • Break a long food menu into sections that guests can scan quickly.
  • Keep item names, descriptions and prices legible on printed pages.
  • Use strong food imagery without making the menu feel heavy or cluttered.
  • Give the printed back cover a useful QR function instead of leaving it as plain branding space.

How the layout was handled

  • Clear food categories were arranged around natural ordering behaviour.
  • Maroon backgrounds carried the restaurant feel, while cream panels protected readability.
  • Prices were aligned into predictable rows so guests do not struggle to compare items.
  • Mockups were prepared to show the menu as a real printed piece before final production.
QR Menu Access

A printed menu with a useful phone-friendly route

The QR code was planned on the back cover so guests can continue from the physical menu into a digital menu view. This is useful where a customer wants to zoom on the menu, share it, or open the full version without waiting for another printed copy.

Questions

What this menu setup means for guests and restaurants

Equinox needed more than a price list. The menu had to feel attractive at the table, easy to read on phone and organised enough for guests to choose without asking too many basic questions.

Why add a QR code to a printed menu?

Some guests want to zoom, share the menu or keep browsing on phone after seeing the printed copy. The QR code on the back cover gives them that option without removing the printed menu from the table.

How do you make a long food list easier to choose from?

We group meals the way customers naturally order: breakfast, light bites, soups, chicken, beef, goat, pizza, burgers, fish and accompaniments. Strong headings, clean spacing and predictable price rows reduce confusion.

Why does the full menu open on another page?

The case study shows the design work, while the digital menu serves real guests. That separation keeps this portfolio page clean and gives Equinox a direct scan destination for customers.

Can Kamog Artistry design this for other restaurants?

Yes. We can prepare a printed menu, phone-friendly QR menu page and PDF version for restaurants, cafés, lounges and hotels. See our restaurant menu design service for the live service page.
