Challenge

A Brand That Needed to Work Clearly Across Multiple Materials

The original identity needed to be refined so it could reproduce properly on stationery, printed materials, signage and reflector wear. The challenge was not only to improve the logo visually, but also to make it more dependable wherever the company needed to use it.

Low-resolution logo source

The existing mark needed a cleaner redraw so it could scale better and hold up across print, signage and uniforms.

Many touchpoints to align

The identity had to feel consistent across office stationery, public-facing display items and branded safety wear.

Professional everyday use

Every output needed to look clear, durable and ready for practical use rather than only looking good in a mockup.

Process

Logo Redraw, Stationery Development, Signage Production and Workwear Branding in One Rollout

The project moved from identity cleanup into print and production. Each stage built on the previous one so the brand could be applied consistently across official documents, office visibility, presentation items and branded reflectors.

Logo redraw

The first step was rebuilding the original logo into a cleaner vector format that could reproduce more reliably.

Stationery rollout

The refined identity moved into letterhead, business cards, company stamp artwork and other official materials.

Office branding

Door labels, signage and a pull up banner extended the identity into office and presentation spaces.

Reflector branding

The final stage applied the brand to reflector wear using DTF production and QR code integration.

Outcome

A More Complete Identity Across Office Materials, Presentation Items and Safety Wear

The final outputs gave Masakaki Engineering a clearer and more connected visual identity. Instead of improving only one symbol, the project delivered a set of materials that now support communication, presentation, office visibility and field use.

Stronger day-to-day consistency

The same identity now carries through documents, cards, signage, banners and reflector wear.

Better presentation in the office

Letterhead, business cards, framed credentials, labels and signage gave the company a more professional presence.

Useful branding in the field

The branded reflectors and QR code feature showed that the identity could work beyond office materials.
