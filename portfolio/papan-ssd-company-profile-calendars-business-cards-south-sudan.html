Challenge

Different Printed Items Needed to Work Together as One Brand Set

PAPAN SSD needed more than one printed item. The work covered a company profile, branded table calendars and business cards, each with its own layout and production needs. At the same time, everything needed to feel connected and professional as one set.

Consistent presentation

The profile, calendars and cards needed to look related, not like separate pieces prepared at different times.

Production readiness

The project needed proper file setup, layout control and output planning across different print formats.

Delivery handling

Since the materials were going to Juba, packaging and dispatch were also part of the work.

This project shows how company profile design, calendar printing, business card printing and print-ready artwork can come together in one complete client project.
Approach

Handled as One Practical Workflow

The work moved through concept development, file preparation, mockups, final production, packaging and dispatch coordination. Each stage supported the next, so the finished materials remained consistent from design through to delivery.

Design and file setup

The company profile, calendars and cards were prepared with layout structure, spacing, bleed setup and output planning in mind from the start.

Mockups and review

Mockups helped show how the materials would look once printed, especially for the profile and the desk calendars.

Printing and finishing

Final outputs were produced across the company profile, table calendars and business cards as one coordinated branded set.

Packaging and dispatch

The finished work was packed, labelled and prepared for delivery to Juba.
