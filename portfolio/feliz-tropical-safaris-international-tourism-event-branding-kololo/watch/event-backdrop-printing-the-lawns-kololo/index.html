Watch the 3 m × 2.4 m Feliz Tropical Safaris event backdrop being printed

The clip shows the 3 m × 2.4 m tourism backdrop being produced before finishing, transport and same-day installation at The Lawns in Kololo. The Embassy of the Republic of Uganda in Moscow badge is visible within the partly printed artwork.

From approved mockup to the physical banner

Before this print run, Kamog Artistry (U) Ltd had rebuilt the supplied embassy mark, completed the tourism-themed backdrop artwork and shared a realistic stand presentation for approval. The printed banner was then finished, delivered and fitted to the reusable display structure before the evening reception.
