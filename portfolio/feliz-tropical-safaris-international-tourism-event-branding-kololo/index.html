Project overview

International tourism event branding built for review, production and real venue use

Feliz Tropical Safaris required a coordinated visual environment for an international guest reception at The Lawns Restaurant in Kololo. The presentation needed to celebrate Uganda’s wildlife, hospitality and destination appeal while remaining credible for Russian and other invited guests, tourism stakeholders, diplomatic representatives and local hospitality partners.
