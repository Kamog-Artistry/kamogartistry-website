UMA 2025 Exhibition Branding in Kampala (TOOKE – BIRDC)

This case study covers end-to-end trade show branding for TOOKE – BIRDC at the UMA 2025 International Trade Expo held at Lugogo (UMA Show Grounds), Kampala. We delivered booth visuals designed for distance readability—then produced, finished, delivered and supported setup so the stand looked complete on event day.

Client / Event

Production Notes

  • Design tools: Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

  • Output: large format printing + mounting where required
  • Fabrication: metal frames + paint finishing for a clean presentation

What Made This Booth Stand Out

Exhibition branding only works when it’s readable from a distance, consistent across every piece, and professionally finished—so the booth looks “complete” even in a crowded trade fair.

Distance readability

We prioritized clear typography, contrast, and spacing so key messaging stays readable in a fast-moving expo environment.

Consistency across touchpoints

Canopy branding + stand frames + panels were aligned to one look, so the booth felt cohesive from every angle.

Engagement that pulls people in

The branded selfie frames created moments visitors could interact with—simple activation that boosts foot traffic and shares.

Results & Impact

Stronger booth visibility

The canopy header and tall stand banners improved “first notice” visibility—helping the brand cut through the noise.

Professional presentation

Clean finishing and frame fabrication ensured the booth looked corporate and organized, not temporary or improvised.

Visitor engagement moments

Photo/selfie frames encouraged interaction—useful for brand recall and shareable content during the expo.
