Project Summary

Client / Project

  • Client: TOOKE – BIRDC
  • Event: UMA 2025 International Trade Expo
  • Location: Lugogo (UMA Show Grounds), Kampala
  • Category: Exhibition Branding & Print Production
  • Approach: Design → Print → Fabrication → Setup support

What We Delivered

  • Canopy / tent branding (high-visibility top banner)
  • Stand banners in custom metal frames
  • Large format booth visuals and panels
  • Branded selfie/photo frames for engagement
  • Production finishing + delivery for setup

Planning an expo? See our Services or send your brief.

Tools & Production

  • Design: Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign
  • Pre-press: correct sizes, safe margins, bleed, and print checks
  • Output: large format printing + panel mounting
  • Fabrication: metal frames + paint finishing

What Made This Project Work

Clear visibility

We designed for distance readability—strong typography, clear contrast, and a consistent TOOKE brand look across all booth touchpoints.

Strong booth presence

Canopy branding + stand frames + large panels created a “complete” look, helping the booth stand out even in a busy expo environment.

Engagement element

The branded selfie frames helped attract visitors and created shareable moments—simple, but powerful for booth traffic.
