Capability statement FAQs

What does the capability statement cover?

It gives a short view of the work Kamog Artistry can execute, including branding, graphic design, print production, signage, event branding, stationery, merchandise and selected website or digital rollout support.

Is there a downloadable capability statement?

Yes. When your internal file needs a PDF copy, use the download button on this page. If the PDF does not answer the full requirement, send the brief, RFQ, BOQ or TOR for proposal review.

Is the capability statement the same as compliance documents?

No. The capability statement explains what Kamog Artistry can execute. Legal, tax, banking and KYC records are handled through the Compliance page and document request routes.

Can the capability statement support procurement review?

Yes. It helps procurement teams check whether Kamog Artistry fits the required supplier category before asking for a quotation, proposal, onboarding step or controlled document.
