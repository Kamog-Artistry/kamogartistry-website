Corporate Profile FAQs

When should procurement teams use this page?

Use it when your team wants to confirm the company identity, review what we can execute, check completed projects and decide whether the next step is a quotation, proposal, profile copy, procurement support or compliance document request.

What is an RFQ?

RFQ means Request for Quotation. Send an RFQ when the items, sizes, quantities, materials and delivery expectations are clear and you mainly need pricing from Kamog Artistry.

What is a BOQ?

BOQ means Bill of Quantities. It lists the required items, measurements, quantities and sometimes specifications so each supplier prices the same scope clearly.

What is a TOR?

TOR means Terms of Reference. It explains the assignment, objectives, deliverables, timeline, submission format and evaluation expectations for a formal response.

Where do we request a formal proposal?

Use Request for Proposal or the proposal form when you have a brief, BOQ, RFQ, TOR, scope note or budget request.

Where are company documents handled?

Legal, tax, banking and KYC records are handled through the Compliance page. This page helps with corporate review, capability and proof of work.

Where can we review completed work?

Review the Portfolio and the proof links on this page before requesting a proposal, supplier onboarding or restricted compliance records.

Can Kamog Artistry respond to institutional procurement requests?

Yes. Share the RFQ, BOQ, TOR, artwork files, delivery location and deadline so we can respond with the right quotation, proposal or production guidance.
