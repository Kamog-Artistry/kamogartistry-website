Who We Are

Kamog Artistry (U) Ltd is a Kampala-based branding, graphic design, printing and signage company built to bridge the gap between strong ideas and clean execution. Many brands do not just need artwork. They need production-ready files, reliable print quality, clear signage, durable branded materials and coordinated delivery.

That is where our model works best. We connect strategy, design and production so businesses do not have to split the same project across disconnected suppliers. From brand development and graphic design to commercial printing, signage and branding, vehicle branding and event branding, the goal is always the same: a cleaner, stronger and more consistent brand presence.

We support SMEs, institutions, NGOs, product brands, retail businesses, corporate teams and event organisers with practical workflows that make approvals easier and final output more dependable.

Quality Standards & Materials

Great branding fails when execution is weak. Our workflow is designed to protect clarity, durability and consistency, especially for signage, retail branding and repeat printing.

Proofing before production

We share layouts and mockups for approval, then prepare production-ready files with correct sizing, bleed and safe margins to reduce errors, reprints and delays. For identity deliverables, start at Branding Kampala.

Material selection for lifespan

Outdoor branding requires correct materials. We advise based on exposure, visibility and installation needs. For outdoor work and fabrication, explore Signage & Branding Kampala.

Finishing & QC checks

Before delivery, we check finishing, alignment, edges and clarity. For brochures, company profiles, signages and marketing print, see Printing Services Kampala.

Consistency across repeat orders

For corporate stationery and repeat printing, we keep controlled templates and export packs so each reprint stays consistent. Explore Branded Stationery Uganda.

How We Work

A simple workflow designed to protect quality, timelines and consistency so you get clean output the first time.

1) Discovery & Brief

You share your goal, location, deadline and quantity or size. We confirm scope and recommend materials for the environment.

You get: a clear scope and guidance on the best approach for your budget.

Related hubs: Branding • Printing • Signage

2) Proofing & Approval

We share layouts and mockups and refine hierarchy, spacing and copy for clarity. You approve before production starts.

You get: production-ready proofs and revisions where needed.

Most requested: Corporate Identity • Brand Guidelines

3) Production & Quality Control

We produce with checks for alignment, finishing, durability and visibility, especially for signage and outdoor branding.

You get: QC-checked output with the right finishing options such as lamination, binding, mounting or fabrication.

Related hubs: Printing Services Kampala • Signage & Branding

4) Delivery / Installation & Support

We coordinate delivery around Kampala, Wakiso and Mukono. Installation support is provided where required, especially for signage and event work.

You get: delivery confirmation and installation guidance or support for long-term use.
